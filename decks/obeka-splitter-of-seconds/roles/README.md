# Obeka role matrix: upkeep resources as the strategy

The premise is that the strategy is "more upkeeps" and the deck's job is resource advantage across several
axes, with synergy modules as plug-ins rather than identities. Four card-scout passes over the legal pool filled
the role files below. This page is the synthesis: how deep each role is, what's worth a slot, which cards fill
two roles, and a slot budget.

- [buffs-and-upkeeps.md](buffs-and-upkeeps.md): connect and connect for more; extra upkeeps; trigger doublers
- [draw-and-mana.md](draw-and-mana.md): upkeep card advantage; upkeep Treasure and mana (with counts)
- [creatures.md](creatures.md): upkeep bodies, ranked with go-wide defence in mind
- [control-and-interaction.md](control-and-interaction.md): upkeep control; interaction that rides the upkeeps

## How deep each role is

| Role | Pool depth (legal Grixis, roughly) | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| Buff Obeka (power) | ~9 compounding counter sources (3 Rings, Feast on the Fallen…), ~10 good static | Deep enough |
| Evasion | ~25 playable | Deep |
| Protection / haste | ~20 / ~10 | Fine |
| Double strike | ~12 playable | A real option |
| **More upkeeps without her** | **5 sources in the whole pool** (Paradox Haze, The Ninth Doctor, Shadow of the Second Sun, Cyclonus) | **Thin: can't remove the dependency** |
| Trigger doublers for her | 11 (Roaming Throne, Wizard's Staff, Gogo, Strionic Resonator, Fractured Realm…) | Amplifies, doesn't reduce dependency |
| Upkeep draw | ~52 pure, ~25 impulse, 5 tutors, ~7 regrowth | Deepest role |
| **Upkeep Treasure / persistent mana** | **6 Treasure makers + 2 persistent pool sources** | **Thin: a bonus, not a role** |
| Batteries (storage lands, Lotus Blossom…) | ~11 | Small but has gems |
| Upkeep creatures | ~20 token makers, ~11 compounding, ~15 reanimators | Deep |
| Upkeep control | ~25 strong finalists across spot, mass, theft, hand, politics | Deep |
| Interaction that rides the upkeeps | delayed removal, verse counters, recursion | Moderate, very distinctive |

**Two honest findings change the plan:**
1. **Treasure on upkeep is rare.** Only six cards make it: Smaug, Bonehoard Dracosaur, Ultimate Green Goblin,
   Generous Plunderer, Tempting Contract and Descent into Avernus. Mana made during an upkeep empties, so
   ramp stays conventional (rocks), and upkeep mana is a bonus. The gems are the persistent kinds:
   - **Storage lands** (Bottomless Vault, Dwarven Hold, Sand Silos) gain a counter in *each* upkeep while
     tapped. They cost no spell slot.
   - **Braid of Fire + Leyline Tyrant:** the Tyrant keeps red mana across steps, so Braid's growing upkeep
     mana reaches main phase 2.
   - **Karn, Legacy Reforged.**
2. **Being "less dependent on her" can't come from more upkeep sources.** There are only four besides her,
   so the dependency is reduced in two other ways:
   - **Every engine must be fine at 1×.** It should earn its slot on turns she doesn't connect.
   - **She must connect more often:** evasion, protection, haste.
   The most useful extra-upkeep fact: Paradox Haze's and The Ninth Doctor's upkeeps come *before* combat, so
   Ring counters gained there help this turn's attack.

## Cards that fill two or more roles (the core of any build)

| Card | Roles | Why it's a two-for-one |
|---|---|---|
| Bonehoard Dracosaur | draw + Treasure + creature | 2 impulse cards and often a Treasure per upkeep; a flying first-strike blocker |
| Avarice Amulet | buff + draw + defence | +2 power and a draw per upkeep; **vigilance lets Obeka attack and still block** |
| Wrecking Ball Arm | buff + evasion | Obeka becomes a base 7/7 (7 upkeeps a hit); creatures with power 2 or less can't block her, which blanks go-wide chump blockers |
| Ring of Valkas | buff + haste | compounding counters; a recast Obeka swings at once |
| Swiftfoot Boots | protection + haste | hexproof, not shroud: our own targeted tricks still work |
| Chronozoa | creatures + defence + lightning rod | flying 3/3s that double on Obeka turns; spot removal backfires |
| Ophiomancer | creatures + defence + fodder | a deathtouch Snake back in *each* upkeep, before every opponent's combat |
| Dragonmaster Outcast | creatures + defence | a 5/5 flier per upkeep once we have 6 lands (the crowd plays it: 78%) |
| Court of Ire | control + reach | damage per upkeep (but the monarch invites attacks) |
| Theater of Horrors | draw + reach | Obeka's hit always satisfies its "an opponent lost life" condition |
| Legacy's Allure / Recantation / Lilting Refrain | control that *charges* per upkeep, cashed at instant speed | steal an attacker / bounce X permanents (tokens vanish) / a counterspell that scales |
| Argentum Masticore | control + body | discard each upkeep to destroy a nonland permanent; protection from multicolored |
| Midnight Banshee | mass control | one-sided against nonblack boards. **It misses Edgar's black Vampire tokens.** |
| Sphere of Annihilation | mass control + graveyard hate | X=0 for {B} exiles every token; on an Obeka turn it fires the same turn |
| Generous Plunderer | mana + body + damage | the opponent's tapped Treasure is a political gift |
| Coercive Portal | control + draw + politics | the table votes each upkeep: a wipe when they need one, a card for us otherwise |

## A slot budget (a starting point, not a list)

| Role | Slots | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Lands | 35 | include 1–2 storage lands and Rogue's Passage |
| Ramp (rocks) | 8 | conventional; upkeep mana is a bonus |
| Connect: buffs / evasion / protection / haste | 6–7 | prefer dual cards: Avarice Amulet, Wrecking Ball Arm, Ring of Valkas, Swiftfoot Boots, Silent Arbiter |
| More upkeeps / doublers | 2–3 | Paradox Haze, The Ninth Doctor, Roaming Throne |
| Upkeep draw | 5–6 | Bonehoard, Theater of Horrors, Tamiyo's Journal, Court of Locthwain, Phyrexian Arena |
| Upkeep creatures | 5–6 | weighted toward defence: Chronozoa, Ophiomancer, Dragonmaster Outcast, Rekindling Phoenix, Virtue of Persistence |
| Upkeep control | 5–6 | Court of Ire, Recantation, Legacy's Allure, Lilting Refrain, Midnight Banshee *or* Sphere of Annihilation, Coercive Portal |
| Instant-speed interaction (not upkeep) | 11–13 | about 5 counters, 4 removal spells, 2–3 alpha-strike answers (Aetherize, Aetherspouts, Blasphemous Act / Toxic Deluge) |
| Conversion module | 3–5 | one place where synergy turns resources into a win (see below) |
| Flex | rest | |

**Density check (do the arithmetic before trusting the budget).** We want two or three upkeep engines on
board by turn 5. By then we've seen about 13–14 cards. With N upkeep engines in the 99, that gives about
13.5·N/99 drawn, so 2.5 needs **N ≈ 18–20**. The role slots above give about 20–24 once the
double-counted cards are removed, which is about right. `./edh engine` can test this goldfish-style once a
list exists.

**Conversion modules** (pick one or two, as plug-ins):
- Ring snowball: commander damage.
- Forgotten Creation + Magmakin Artillerist: discards become damage.
- Endless Evil on Dragonmaster Outcast: dragons grow quadratically.
- Mechanized Production on Thopter Assembly: a 5/5 flier per upkeep; eight copies win.
- Court of Ire with copies.
- Descent into Avernus as a kill switch on a big turn: it hits everyone.

## Cautions the scouts surfaced
- **Life budget:** Dark Confidant-style cards cost about 8–12 life on an Obeka turn. Demonic Pact reaches
  "you lose the game" around the fourth upkeep. Mind Unbound and The Magic Mirror deck us in about two
  Obeka turns.
- **Crumbling Ashes fails against +1/+1-counter boards:** the counters annihilate. Harbinger of Night's -1/-1
  counters erase our Ring counters.
- **Shroud (Greaves, Whispersilk Cloak) blocks our own targeted tricks**, so prefer hexproof. Access Tunnel
  stops working once she grows past 3 power.
- **Unverified rules question:** does an extra combat created at declare attackers (Karlach, Genji Glove)
  come *after* Obeka's upkeeps, which are created at combat damage? The scout cited rule 500.8 from memory.
  Check the comprehensive rules or a judge before building around it.
