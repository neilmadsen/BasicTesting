# Obeka, Splitter of Seconds: strategy map (Bracket 3)

**Obeka, Splitter of Seconds** {1}{U}{B}{R}, 2/5 menace. *Whenever Obeka deals combat damage to a
player, you get that many additional upkeep steps after this phase.*

## Reading the card

The obvious reading is "upkeep triggers, times N", and the crowd builds exactly that: Courts, the Ring
cycle (which also grows Obeka, so the next hit gives more upkeeps), monarch, As Foretold and suspend, and
Dragonmaster Outcast. In EDHREC's 1,421 bracket-3 decks, the top 25 non-land cards are almost all that pile.
It works, but it plays the same game every time: suit up, connect, and let triggers resolve.

The second-order readings:

1. **An extra upkeep is "your next upkeep".** Rebound ("at the beginning of your next upkeep, you may cast
   this card from exile without paying its mana cost") fires in the extra upkeep of the *same turn*. So
   does every other "your next upkeep" delayed trigger. Every rebound spell, and under Cast Through Time
   every instant and sorcery, cast before combat damage resolves twice in the turn Obeka connects. The
   crowd plays no rebound cards with Obeka at all.
2. **Upkeep triggers can be spells, removal, theft and reanimation, not just value.** Galvanoth and
   Panoptic Mirror cast a free spell every upkeep. Virtue of Persistence reanimates from *any* graveyard
   every upkeep, Culling Scales destroys every upkeep, Frenzied Fugue steals every upkeep, and Demonic Pact
   runs through all its modes in one turn. Under Obeka these are a control deck's repeatable answers,
   multiplied. Almost none of them appear on Obeka's EDHREC page.
3. **The extra upkeeps come after combat, and there's no extra draw step.** Anything that needs the
   upkeeps *before* attacking (pump, haste, equip) doesn't get them that turn. Mana empties between steps,
   so instants cast in an upkeep need their mana that step. Sagas don't tick (lore counters come in the
   precombat main phase), and "the next turn's upkeep" cantrips don't fire early.
4. **Upkeep drawbacks multiply too.** Cumulative upkeep, echo, vanishing, "sacrifice unless you pay",
   "each player's upkeep" punishers, and pacts cast before combat are all paid several times, or right away.

What stops her: she has to connect. A 2/5 menace attacker needs evasion (unblockable, protection),
blocker removal, or a table with no good double blocks. She's a four-mana, three-colour commander that
removal hits cleanly, and graveyard hate blanks the reanimation readings.

## Archetypes considered

| Archetype | Thesis | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| **Twice-Told** (rebound spellbook control) | Instants and sorceries with rebound, Cast Through Time, Galvanoth and Panoptic Mirror. Each removal and draw spell cast before damage happens twice when Obeka connects; without her it's a normal draw-go control deck. | **Build.** It's the creative build: a mechanic the crowd doesn't touch, interactive by construction, with more than one way to win (doubled burn to each opponent, Obeka chip damage, copy-Obeka turns). |
| **The Long Morning** (upkeep-permanent control) | Removal, theft and reanimation that trigger each upkeep (Virtue of Persistence, Culling Scales, Court of Ire, Frenzied Fugue, Demonic Pact with a way out), behind counterspells. Every Obeka hit is two or three answers. | **Build.** It's the interactive build: a permanent-based engine, different cards and a different play pattern from Twice-Told. It wins by turning the table's threats into ours, with Court of Ire and Demonic Pact as reach. |
| Crowd upkeep pile (Courts, Rings, monarch, Outcast, As Foretold) | Suit Obeka up, connect, let triggers resolve. | **Rejected as a build.** It's one-note and well documented; `audit omissions` compares each build with it instead. Its best cards (Court of Ire, a ring or two, the evasion package) can appear in either build where they're the best card for the slot. |
| Suspend / time counters (As Foretold, Jhoira, Ancestral Vision) | Upkeeps remove time counters, so suspended spells arrive fast and free. | **Rejected.** It's a crowd sub-theme (As Foretold is in 83% of decks), slow to set up, and its payoffs are random. Rebound gives the same "free spell in upkeep" with control over timing. |
| Extra combats (Port Razer, Relentless Assault, Bloodthirster) | Connect again for another batch of upkeeps. | **Rejected.** It's aggro and one-note, close to an extra-turn feel at bracket 3, and it only helps when Obeka connects twice. |
| Herald of Leshrac land theft | Cumulative upkeep that steals a land per age counter: with three upkeeps, six lands in one turn. | **Rejected.** Functionally mass land denial, against the spirit of bracket 3. Noted as a cute interaction only. |
| Demonic Pact + Donate kill | Fire all three good modes in one Obeka turn, then hand the pact to an opponent. | **Folded into The Long Morning** as one win path, not a deck. It's a deterministic two-card kill of one player (bracket 3 REVIEW: not infinite, and it needs Obeka's turn or several turns). |
