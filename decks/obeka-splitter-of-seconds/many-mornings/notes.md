# Many Mornings: notes (Obeka, Splitter of Seconds, Bracket 3)

## Plan

The deck spreads its upkeep value across six resources instead of one engine: cards (Bonehoard Dracosaur,
Theater of Horrors, Plargg and Nassari, Phyrexian Arena, Taigam, Avarice Amulet, Super Intelligence), defensive
bodies (Ophiomancer, Chronozoa, Rekindling Phoenix, Dragonmaster Outcast, Shrine of Loyal Legions, Virtue of
Persistence), control that charges each upkeep and cashes at instant speed (Recantation, Legacy's Allure,
Lilting Refrain, Torch Song), repeatable upkeep damage (Court of Ire), a delayed sweeper (Sphere of
Annihilation), Obeka's own power (three Rings), a little
mana (Replicating Ring, Bonehoard Treasure, Bottomless Vault) and the multiplier package. Around it sits a
cheap instant-speed Grixis shell: five counters, six spot answers, and nine board answers.

Upkeep engine count: 24 cards in the 99 have a trigger worded "at the beginning of your upkeep" or "at the
beginning of each upkeep". Paradox Haze (an extra upkeep on each of our turns) and Super Intelligence (a draw in each of our
upkeeps) use other wording, for 26 upkeep cards in all.

**Ways to win**
1. **Commander damage.** Wrecking Ball Arm makes her a base 7/7 and Hard-Won Jitte or Psychotic Fury gives
   double strike: 14 a hit, so two hits kill. Rings and Avarice Amulet add on top. Psychic Paper, Brotherhood
   Regalia (unblockable, ward {2}), Key to the City, Silent Arbiter and Rogue's Passage get her through.
2. **Dragons.** With six or more lands, Dragonmaster Outcast (doubled by Harmonic Prodigy, copied by Endless Evil)
   makes a 5/5 flying Dragon in each of our upkeeps. These win the long game after a sweeper.
3. **Burn reach.** Court of Ire (7 a shot as monarch), Torch Song's verse counters,
   Delayed Blast Fireball and Theater of Horrors' ping finish a table that's been held off.
4. **Attrition.** Out-card the table behind counters and sweepers; Virtue of Persistence (reanimates from any graveyard each
   upkeep) and Legacy's Allure (steals a creature) make their best creatures ours.

**Key turns.** T1–2: a rock, a 2-mana engine (Legacy's Allure, Lilting Refrain, Shrine, a Ring) or Outcast.
T3: Ophiomancer, Theater or Paradox Haze, or hold up a counter. T4: Obeka with protection mana, or Obeka plus
a 1-mana equip. T5+: equip, connect, and resolve the upkeeps before main phase 2.

**Mulligan for:** 3–4 lands (or 2 lands + a 2-mana rock) and at least one of: a cheap upkeep engine, a piece
of evasion/protection, or a cheap counter. Ship 1-landers even with good spells: 30 of the 63 nonland cards
are 2-drops, but the deck still wants four lands by T4. Don't keep a hand whose only action is 5-drops.

## Pilot notes

**Key lines**
- *Standard connect:* main phase 1, cast any upkeep permanent you hold (it fires in this turn's extra upkeeps),
  equip Obeka (evasion first, then Arm/Jitte), attack. Each extra upkeep resolves after combat and before main
  phase 2; spend Treasures and impulse cards in main phase 2.
- *Wizard line:* Psychic Paper on Obeka: choose a harmless creature card name (not a legend we control) and type **Wizard**. Wizard's Staff then
  equips for {1}. Roaming Throne always names **Wizard**. With Paper + Staff + Throne she triggers three times:
  a 2-damage hit is 6 upkeeps. Arcane Adaptation (name Wizard) is the second hub: it also makes Bonehoard,
  Dragonmaster and Chronozoa Wizards, so Throne and Prodigy double them.
- *Harmonic Prodigy without a hub:* doubles Dragonmaster Outcast (Shaman) and Taigam (Wizard) natively. Prodigy +
  Outcast at six lands is two Dragons per upkeep.
- *Double strike:* Jitte (equip {2}) or Psychotic Fury after blockers (instant, cantrip). Double strike plus Staff
  is four triggers. With Arm that's 14 commander damage and 28 upkeeps: **don't do this with Avarice Amulet on
  her and a short library** (see "never").
- *Paradox Haze:* the extra upkeep is before combat, so Ring counters land before the attack and every engine
  runs twice even when she doesn't connect.
- *Charge-and-cash:* let Recantation, Legacy's Allure, Lilting Refrain and Torch Song sit. Each gains a counter per
  upkeep, so an Obeka hit for N adds N more (2 bare, 7 with Wrecking Ball Arm, double that with a doubler). Recantation ({U}, sacrifice) then bounces up to X permanents at instant speed in an opponent's combat (tokens cease to exist);
  Allure steals their biggest attacker; Refrain counters a wipe for zero mana.
- *Plargg and Nassari:* in each of our upkeeps every player exiles to a nonland card; an opponent denies one and we
  cast up to two of the rest free. On an Obeka turn that's two free spells per extra upkeep, the only real mana the
  extra upkeeps give us. Cast it main phase 1 before the attack. Under Arcane Adaptation it's a Wizard, so Throne
  and Prodigy double it. Free spells that need targets (removal from an opponent's deck) are cast in the upkeep, so
  aim them before main phase 2.
- *Endless Evil* goes on Dragonmaster Outcast (with six or more lands, each 1/1 copy makes its own Dragon) or on Bonehoard Dracosaur.
- *Sphere of Annihilation:* X=0 for {B} exiles every token at our next upkeep; cast it in main phase 1 of a turn
  Obeka will connect and it fires the same turn. X=3 clears 1–3-drops and spares her.

**Hold up**
- From T3 onward keep {U}{U} or {U}{B} up for Counterspell / Drown in the Loch whenever the tap-out play isn't an
  engine. Fierce Guardianship and Deadly Rollick and Deflecting Swat are free with Obeka out: they're why you can
  tap out on Obeka turns.
- Against Edgar or Giada, from T5 keep one board answer in hand rather than casting a second engine:
  Aetherize/Aetherspouts/Delayed Blast Fireball/Cyclonic Rift at instant speed, Recantation charged on board.
- Shrine of Loyal Legions: crack it in an opponent's declare-attackers step for Myr blockers, not on our turn.
- Ring of Evos Isle's {2} hexproof and Ring of Xathrid's {2} regenerate on opponents' turns when she's the target.

**Answer on sight**
- Edgar Markov's or Giada's board once it reaches about 4 attackers: Witch's Vengeance (name Vampire or Angel; we run
  neither type), Delayed Blast Fireball, Toxic Deluge (X=4 spares an undamaged Obeka),
  Blasphemous Act (regenerate her with Ring of Xathrid first).
- Anthems and token doublers that make a go-wide board lethal (Edgar's lords, Anointed Procession-type cards).
- Graveyard hate on a stack while Virtue is the plan; Rest in Peace blanks Virtue and Taigam's -X/-X.
- Anything that steals or copies our Equipment (Avarice Amulet goes to an opponent if the equipped creature dies).

**Never**
- Never stack every multiplier when the library is small: Avarice Amulet's draw is mandatory and doublers double
  it. Count upkeeps × draws per upkeep against the library before adding Staff/Throne/double strike.
- Never name Ogre or Warlock with Roaming Throne in this list; never choose a type other than Wizard on Paper.
- Never attack Obeka into two untapped blockers without evasion (menace only needs two chumps).
- Never become the monarch with a fragile board unless Court of Ire's 7-a-turn is worth the attacks it draws.
- Never cast Toxic Deluge for X≥5 with a 2/5 Obeka unless she has Ring counters or you accept losing her.
- Don't take Phyrexian Arena life loss lightly on a 7-upkeep turn: that's 7 life.

## Key cards and lines

1. **Psychic Paper**: unblockable + ward + the Wizard hub for Staff, Throne and Prodigy.
2. **Wizard's Staff**: doubles every trigger on her, including the draw Avarice Amulet grants.
3. **Wrecking Ball Arm**: 7 upkeeps a hit and 1/1 tokens can't block her.
4. **Bonehoard Dracosaur** and **Plargg and Nassari**: two cards plus Treasure/Dinosaur per upkeep on a 5/5 first-strike flier; two free spells per upkeep on a 5/4.
5. **Recantation**: the scaling instant-speed answer to a token board.
6. **Dragonmaster Outcast + Harmonic Prodigy (+ Endless Evil)**: the second, non-Obeka win.
7. **Silent Arbiter**: one attacker per combat blanks Edgar's and Giada's alpha strikes, and menace Obeka can't be
   blocked. It binds our Dragons too, so cast it when defence matters more than a Dragon swing.
8. **Delayed Blast Fireball / Witch's Vengeance**: Fireball is one-sided; Vengeance is typal (it hits every
   creature of the named type, but we run no Angels and one Vampire with 4 toughness): the answers to the two named
   go-wide threats.

Hit rate: 13 of 19 gem better, 4 staple better, 2 split (68%).

**Defences of the four "staple better" verdicts.** The judge compared each gem with a Court and with Damnation in
isolation. Three of them lose to a Court: the brief allows at most one Court, because the monarch draws the attacks
that killed us in the piloted games, and Court of Ire already holds that slot. Among non-Court options, Theater is
the best upkeep card source at 3 mana, and Phoenix and Shrine are the defensive bodies that survive or ambush a
go-wide swing. Witch's Vengeance vs Damnation: Damnation kills Obeka (commander tax) and all our upkeep blockers,
and the deck already runs two symmetric resets (Toxic Deluge, Blasphemous Act). The named threats are typal, so a
one-sided typal sweeper is a third answer of a different kind, not a fourth reset.

## Omitted staples

- **As Foretold (79%)**: free spells by time counters. Our free spells are already the commander-gated ones; slot went to the doubler package.
- **Court of Ambition (73%), Court of Vantress (68%), Court of Cunning (46%)**: one Court max (monarch invites attacks); Court of Ire holds it.
- **Plargg and Nassari (61%)**: included (user correction; it replaced Twilight Prophet). Only the exile is symmetric: each player exiles to a nonland card, an opponent denies one, and we alone cast up to two of the rest free. That's two free spells per upkeep with no mana, which matters because extra upkeeps give no mana. A core upkeep card-advantage engine here; my first draft misread it.
- **Twilight Prophet (79%)**: cut for Plargg and Nassari; its draw waits on the city's blessing (ten permanents).
- **Extravagant Replication (61%)**: 6 mana; Endless Evil does the copy job at 3 on the one target that matters (Outcast). First swap-in if the curve allows.
- **Mechanized Production (60%)**: the Thopter Assembly module needs two cards and 10 mana; the deck wins via commander damage and Dragons instead.
- **Exotic Orchard (60%), Crumbling Necropolis (53%), Stormcarved Coast (42%)**: replaced by fetches, original duals, shocks and Xander's Lounge.
- **Inspiring Refrain (55%), Rousing Refrain (46%), Profane Tutor (40%), Suspended Sentence (45%), Jhoira of the Ghitu (46%)**: suspend sub-theme; this deck is upkeep permanents, not suspend.
- **Nightscape Familiar (52%)**: cost reduction for blue/red spells; our curve is already 2.8.
- **Thassa, God of the Sea (51%)**: evasion via scry/blink; replaced by Psychic Paper, Regalia and Key, which don't need devotion.

- **Skyline Despot (43%)**: 7 mana and monarch-dependent; Dragonmaster Outcast makes the same Dragons for 1.

## Testing

**Goldfish (10,000 trials, 35 lands + 8 rocks).** Obeka cast on curve (T4) 84%, by T5 91%, average T3.8. Screw
(≤2 lands on T4) 8%. Flood (≥9 lands by T7) 2%. Average nonland MV 2.81; thirty 2-drops.

**Engine goldfish** (one draw a turn, no upkeep draws modelled, so these are floors for access):

| Block | by | Online by its turn | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| Obeka armed (her + 2 upkeep pieces + evasion) | T6 | 51% (T7 59%) | bottleneck: evasion not drawn 33% (6 castable pieces + Rogue's Passage, which the sim can't count) |
| Two upkeep engines on board | T5 | 73% (T4 60%) | 24 regex matches |
| Doubled hit | T7 | 56% | counts Throne and Prodigy as doublers even without a Wizard hub |
| Board answer held | T7 | **78%** (T5 72%) | 9 answers + Demonic Tutor |
| Interaction held up (2 cheap answers) | T5 | 87% | |

Accepted targets and why: board answers 78% by T7 meets the user's "majority by T7" with margin. Two engines by T5 at
73% is the thesis and is met. Obeka armed at 51% by T6 is accepted because the sim ignores all upkeep draw, Rogue's
Passage and Silent Arbiter's real effect, and because the deck functions without her connecting. Doubled hit: a
stricter run counting only unconditional doublers (Staff, Jitte, Psychotic Fury, with Demonic Tutor) is 42% by T7,
and Throne/Prodigy *with* a hub is 8% by T7; the doublers are a multiplier on a deck that already works at 1×, so
about half of games by T7 is the intended rate, not a gap.

**Audits.**
- Omissions: 20 `!!` staples, all answered above.
- Claims: first pass 64 checked, 49 ok, 11 overstated, 4 wrong; all 15 fixed. Real corrections: Ring of Xathrid
  regenerates through Blasphemous Act but not Toxic Deluge; Key to the City must be used before blockers; Roaming
  Throne names Wizard here, so it reaches her only while Paper or Adaptation makes her one; Lilting Refrain stays a
  soft counter. Passes over deck.txt + notes.md found 23 more (mostly "each upkeep" meaning only our upkeeps, and
  the six-lands condition on Dragonmaster Outcast), all fixed. One auditor verdict rejected: it said Witch's
  Vengeance naming Vampire kills a 2/4 Twilight Prophet (it doesn't; moot now that Prophet is cut). Final pass: 104
  claims, 97 ok; the remaining 7 were fixed afterwards (Plargg is "up to" two spells; Recantation tokens cease to
  exist rather than die; Key to the City is once a turn at a card's cost; Taigam replaces the draw step).
- Gems: 19 judged, 68% hit rate; four "staple better" defended above.

**Forge smoke test.** 16 games vs the bracket-3 gauntlet, run on the list *before* the Plargg and Nassari swap
(Twilight Prophet in its slot): **0 wins of 16 (95% CI 0–19%, baseline 25%)**, 3 timeouts, 14 deaths by life total,
average death round 10.7. Obeka was cast in 94% of games, first on round 5.3 (goldfish T3.8; opponents' removal and
the AI's sequencing). The engine itself works in Forge: logs show her connecting, the trigger resolving, Avarice
Amulet and Rings equipped, and stacked triggers on later hits. What fails is defence: the AI can't cast five of the
deck's answers (flagged unplayable for the AI: Recantation, Toxic Deluge, Rakdos Charm, Taigam, Dimir Signet) and
rarely finds instant-speed windows, so the deck plays as a goldfish with no sweepers and dies to the board, which is
the known weakness. Read as "functions, sim blind to the control half", not as a strength estimate. The piloted runs
are the real test of the go-wide plan.

Never cast (12) and verdicts:
- Recantation, Toxic Deluge, Rakdos Charm, Taigam, Dimir Signet: AI-flagged; sim is blind. Keep.
- Aetherspouts, Deflecting Swat, Psychotic Fury: instant-speed tricks the AI rarely times. Keep.
- Shrine of Loyal Legions: activation is the whole card; the AI cast it 0 times. Keep, but it's the first flex slot to
  revisit if the piloted games don't use it either.
- Brotherhood Regalia: the AI equipped Rings and Amulet instead; redundancy, not a bad card. Keep.
- Endless Evil: needs Dragonmaster Outcast on board to be good; the AI never had the pair. Keep.
- Super Intelligence: the AI never cast a one-mana Aura on its own creature. Weakest upkeep draw card; second flex
  slot to revisit.

## Bracket

`./edh validate --bracket 3`: PASS. Game Changers 3/3: Cyclonic Rift, Fierce Guardianship, Demonic Tutor. No
mass land denial, no extra turns. Commander Spellbook's estimate is "Powerful (≈bracket 3+)" from combos alone;
no two-card infinite is in the list. The multiplier stack (Paper + Staff + Throne + double strike) is not infinite:
it's a large finite number of upkeeps that needs four pieces and Obeka connecting, realistically T7+. It is the
part of the deck that can feel above bracket 3 on a table without interaction; on such a table, put Staff on a
different creature rather than stacking all four.

## Swaps considered

- **Strionic Resonator**: was in the first draft as the fifth doubler; Hard-Won Jitte replaced it because double
  strike also doubles commander damage. Swap back if you want a doubler that copies Court of Ire or verse triggers.
- **Extravagant Replication** (copy Bonehoard or Roaming Throne each upkeep): the strongest 6-drop left out.
- **Mirror Room // Fractured Realm**: a global doubler at 7; too slow for this curve.
- **Perplexing Test / Consume the Meek / Kozilek's Return**: more instant go-wide answers if the meta is all tokens.
- **Twilight Prophet**: cut for Plargg and Nassari; swap back for a flier blocker if the meta is all fliers.
- **Feast on the Fallen**: cut for Key to the City; a fourth Ring-like grower.
- **Gogo, Master of Mimicry / Echo, Perceptive Prodigy**: activated trigger copiers; mana-hungry.
- **Blue Mage's Cane / Black Mage's Rod**: a third Wizard hub as Equipment if Adaptation keeps getting removed.
- **Bitterblossom / Thopter Spy Network**: more flying chump bodies; life cost per upkeep for Bitterblossom.
- **The Ninth Doctor**: a second pre-combat extra upkeep source alongside Paradox Haze.

## Revision 2 (after the first Jev vs Haiku paired games)
- **Bottomless Vault → Swamp.** In a piloted game the strategist led with the Vault ("it gains a storage counter
  each upkeep") over three untapped duals and we were a land behind all game. A storage land is greed this
  deck can't afford early.
- **Key to the City → Thassa, God of the Sea** and **Shrine of Loyal Legions → As Foretold.** Both are crowd
  staples (51% and 83% of Obeka decks) that the first build left out on misread reasons (Thassa as a "blink"
  card; As Foretold as "free spells from time counters" we didn't need). As Foretold charges in every upkeep
  and makes one spell free on every player's turn: a free counter or removal each round. Thassa is
  indestructible repeatable evasion for {1}{U} and a scry in every upkeep.
- Pilot changes in the same round: keywords (including until-end-of-turn ones) and WIN CONDITION flags are
  shown on permanents and targets; the strategist's prompt gained a tempo rule (spend reactive mana when nobody
  is pressing us, lands that make mana first, free attacks) and a win-condition rule.
