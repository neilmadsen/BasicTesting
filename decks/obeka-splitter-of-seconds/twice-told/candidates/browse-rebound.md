# Browse: /\brebound\b/ (22 cards, all unlisted on Obeka's EDHREC page)
Rule check: rebound fires at "your next upkeep" -> Obeka's extra upkeep the same turn. Free cast => X = 0.

Take
- Cast Through Time (7) — every instant/sorcery from hand gains rebound; the deck's doubling engine.
- Ojer Pakpatiq (4) — instants from hand gain rebound; a cheaper, creature half of CTT; returns as land if killed.
- Faithless Salvaging (2) — rebound loot; cheap, instant, fuels Galvanoth set-up and Archmage draws.
- Staggershock (3) — 2 dmg twice: kills two x/2 blockers/dorks, or two pings to face/walkers.
- Consuming Vapors (4) — edict twice; gains life; answers hexproof/voltron.
- Recurring Insight (6) — draw = opponent's hand size, twice.
- Quantum Misalignment (5) — non-legendary token copy of Obeka (or Galvanoth/Archmage), twice.
- Distortion Strike (1) — makes Obeka unblockable precombat; cheapest evasion in the deck.
- Fantastic Elasticity (3) — bounce a blocker, then on rebound regrow a spent spell (or bounce again).
- Into the Time Vortex (5) — two cascades (the rebound cast also cascades).
- World at War (5) — extra combat; its rebound in Obeka's extra upkeep adds a SECOND extra combat after main 2. Finisher.

Considered, cut
- Void Squall (5) — double bounce, but 5 mana sorcery tempo only; Fantastic Elasticity does it for 3.
- Blessed Reincarnation (4) — exile twice, but each gives the opponent a random creature; bad trade in EDH.
- Sight Beyond Sight (4) — two picks-of-two for 4; too little effect per mana.
- Taigam's Strike (4) — Distortion Strike does it for 1.
- Transpose (3) — loot + 0/1 pinger token; fine, below the cut.
- Surreal Memoir (4) — random regrowth.
- Ojutai's Breath, Ojutai's Summons, Virulent Swipe, Flame On! — low impact.
- Fevered Suspicion (8) — too expensive.
