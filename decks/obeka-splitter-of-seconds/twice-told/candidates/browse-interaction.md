# Browse: cheap instant interaction (counters <=2, removal <=3)
Counters: Counterspell, Arcane Denial, Swan Song, Negate, Fierce Guardianship (GC, free with Obeka out), Mana Drain (GC),
  Izzet Charm (counter/2 dmg/loot — good twice), Drown in the Loch, Muddle the Mixture (transmute for 2-drops), Spell Pierce.
Removal that is good twice under rebound: Kolaghan's Command, Prismari Command, Electrolyze, Fire Covenant, Bedevil, Terminate,
  Lightning Bolt, Infernal Grasp, Chaos Warp, Feed the Swarm (sorcery, enchantments).
Note: counterspells don't double (their rebound arrives in our upkeep with nothing to counter); removal and draw do.
