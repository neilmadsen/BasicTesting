# Obeka, Splitter of Seconds: Twice-Told (Bracket 3)

A Grixis spellbook control deck. When Obeka deals combat damage, she grants that many extra upkeep steps
after combat, and each of them counts as "your next upkeep". So every rebound spell cast before combat
damage is cast again that turn, and Galvanoth and Panoptic Mirror each fire once per upkeep. On turns she
doesn't connect, the deck plays draw-go: hold up a counter or removal, trade one for one, and draw ahead.

## Plan

**How it wins (four routes, the same engine used differently):**
1. **Mirror burn.** Imprint Delayed Blast Fireball (X = 3) or Fiery Confluence (X = 4) on Panoptic Mirror.
   The Mirror's copy is created in exile and cast from there, so Fireball deals 5 to each opponent and each
   creature they control, every upkeep. On an Obeka turn with two extra upkeeps that's 15 to each opponent
   plus the natural upkeep's 5 the turn before. Fiery Confluence is 6 to each opponent per upkeep.
2. **Doubled burn turn.** With Cast Through Time (or Ojer Pakpatiq for instants) out, cast Breath of Malfegor,
   Maestros Charm (drain mode) and Fiery Confluence from hand before combat, connect with Obeka, and recast the
   rebounding ones free in her first extra upkeep (all three with Cast Through Time; only the two instants with
   Ojer). Guttersnipe adds 2 to each opponent for every one of those casts.
3. **World at War turn.** Cast World at War before combat. Obeka connects, and in her first extra upkeep World
   at War rebounds, which sets up a *second* additional combat after main phase 2. That's three combats in all,
   and every one Obeka connects in grants more upkeeps (more Mirror copies, and more Galvanoth looks, which cast
   only when the top card is an instant or sorcery). It's the finisher once the table is low or tapped out.
   Quantum Misalignment's non-legendary Obeka tokens are summoning sick the turn they're made; they attack
   on later turns.
4. **Control attrition.** Rebound removal kills two things per card, Theater of Horrors (on turns an opponent
   has lost life) and Archmage Emeritus generate cards, and Obeka chips for 2 (more with Sword or Combat
   Research) while Guttersnipe and the Transpose Wizard ping. Slow, but it's what happens when the engine
   pieces get answered.

**Key turns.** T1–3: land drops, a rock, cheap selection, and hold up a counter or removal. T4: Obeka (84% on
curve in the goldfish). T5–6: evasion on Obeka, then the first "armed" attack with a rebound spell cast
precombat. T6+: Panoptic Mirror or Cast Through Time, protected by a held counter or Fierce Guardianship.

**Mulligan.** Keep 3–4 lands with at least two colours, plus either a rock or two-mana interaction. Ideal
keep: lands, a rock, one evasion piece or engine card, one counter. Mulligan 1-landers and 6-landers, and
hands with no blue source (40% of our pips are blue). Don't keep a hand of only five-drops.

## Pilot notes

**The order of an Obeka turn (this matters):**
1. *Main phase 1:* cast from hand the spells you want doubled: rebound cards, or any instant or sorcery with
   Cast Through Time out. Ojer Pakpatiq gives rebound only to instants cast from hand. Put evasion on Obeka here (Aqueous Form, Psychic Paper,
   Key to the City ({T}, discard a card), Aether Tunnel, or Thassa, God of the Sea's {1}{U} unblockable ability). Pumps such as Sword of Wealth and Power, Combat
   Research and Aether Tunnel must land *before* combat, because each point of power is another upkeep.
2. *Combat:* Obeka connects. Sword of Wealth and Power, if equipped, makes a Treasure and sets up a copy of the
   next instant or sorcery we cast this turn, which will be the first rebound cast.
3. *Extra upkeeps (after combat):* order the triggers in each one. Put Thassa's scry and a rebounding
   Brainstorm *before* Galvanoth's look, so the top card is chosen. Rebounds all fire in the *first* extra
   upkeep. Mana empties between steps, so any mana you tap in an upkeep must be spent in that upkeep. An
   instant cast from hand in extra upkeep 1 (with Cast Through Time or Ojer) rebounds again in extra upkeep 2.
4. *Main phase 2:* sorcery-speed follow-up with leftover lands and the Treasures made in the upkeeps (Storm-Kiln
   Artist, Unexpected Windfall, Big Score, Prismari Command). In main phase 2, cast the sorceries Theater of Horrors exiled and play an exiled land if
   you still have a land drop (once Obeka has connected, an opponent has lost life this turn); its instants can
   be cast during the upkeeps themselves.

**Key lines.**
- *Mirror lock:* Panoptic Mirror, then {3},{T}: imprint Delayed Blast Fireball. From then on each upkeep is a
  one-sided 5-damage sweep plus 5 to each opponent. Imprint at the end of an opponent's turn to dodge sorcery-
  speed answers.
- *Cast Through Time turn:* CTT, then next turn a pile of cheap instants and sorceries from hand precombat
  (Staggershock, Electrolyze, Kolaghan's Command, Unexpected Windfall), Obeka connects, and each one that
  resolved may be cast again once, in her first extra upkeep, without paying its mana cost (Windfall still
  needs a discard). The rebound cast is from exile, so it doesn't rebound a third time.
- *Ojer Pakpatiq:* on opponents' turns, removal and draw instants we cast from hand rebound in our next upkeep.
  The free card arrives even without Obeka connecting.
- *World at War:* cast it precombat only when Obeka can connect (evasion on), since the rebound and the second
  extra combat both depend on that first hit.
- *Quantum Misalignment:* copy Obeka (non-legendary token, menace, same trigger); the rebound makes a second.
  Copy Galvanoth instead if Obeka is under pressure: every Galvanoth copy triggers in every upkeep, though each
  sees the same top card unless the previous trigger cast it (so stack the top with Brainstorm, Top or Thassa).

**Hold up.** On opponents' turns keep 2 mana up for Counterspell, Arcane Denial, Negate, Drown in the Loch or
instant removal. With Obeka in play, Fierce Guardianship is free, so you can tap out for Cast Through Time or
Mirror and still counter one noncreature spell aimed at it (it can't stop abilities or creature ETBs). Save Swan Song (enchantment, instant or sorcery only) and Negate (noncreature) for
wipes and removal spells aimed at Obeka or the engine; neither stops creature-based or ability-based removal.

**Answer on sight.** Graveyard and exile hate doesn't hurt us much; what hurts is (a) creatures that can
double-block Obeka (menace) when she lacks evasion: clear them precombat with Staggershock (toughness 2 or
less) or Fire Covenant (X damage split for X life); (b) artifact
or enchantment removal aimed at Mirror or Cast Through Time; (c) stax that taxes spells (Rule of Law effects)
or stops extra steps; (d) anything that wins faster than our Mirror clock.

**Never do.**
- Don't cast X spells as rebound or Mirror targets: a free cast has X = 0 (Memory Deluge also looks at 0 cards).
- Don't count on "the next turn's upkeep" cantrips firing in Obeka's extra upkeeps: they don't.
- Don't attack Obeka into a board that kills her without a counter or protection up when the payoff is small.
- Don't use rebound card-draw on an opponent with an empty hand (Recurring Insight targets one opponent).
- Don't tap the Mirror's imprint mana in an upkeep expecting to use it in main phase 2; it empties.

## Key cards and lines

1. **Panoptic Mirror + Delayed Blast Fireball / Fiery Confluence**: the main clock; a copy every upkeep.
2. **Cast Through Time**: instants and sorceries cast from hand that resolve before Obeka connects are cast
   again in that turn's first extra upkeep.
3. **Galvanoth**: each upkeep, may cast the top card free if it's an instant or sorcery; Thassa, Aqueous Form,
   Brainstorm and Top help set it up.
4. **Ojer Pakpatiq**: a 4-mana Cast Through Time for instants that comes back as a land if it dies.
5. **Sword of Wealth and Power**: Obeka hits for 4 (4 upkeeps), protection from instants and sorceries, and a
   copy of the first rebound cast.
6. **World at War**: cast from hand precombat; if Obeka connects in the first combat, its rebound in the extra
   upkeep adds a second extra combat, for three in all.
7. **Archmage Emeritus / Storm-Kiln Artist** (magecraft: cast or copy) and **Guttersnipe** (cast only):
   payoffs that turn each upkeep cast into a card, a Treasure, or 2 damage to each opponent.
8. **Theater of Horrors**: a card per upkeep that stays available on later turns.
9. **Court of Ire**: 2 damage in each of our upkeeps, 7 while we're the monarch: removal or reach that Obeka's
   extra upkeeps multiply, and her menace hits help take the monarchy back.

  can still target Obeka. *Gem better.*

## Omitted staples

- **Twilight Prophet** (79%): needs ten permanents for city's blessing, and this deck runs few permanents. Its upkeep drain is the Long Morning build's job.
- **As Foretold** (79%): suspend gives random, delayed free spells; rebound and Galvanoth do that on our schedule.
- **Dragonmaster Outcast** (75%): needs six lands and dies to everything; tokens don't fit a control deck.
- **Replicating Ring** (74%): ramp rock that pays off slowly; plain rocks and Treasures do the job here.
- **Court of Ambition / Court of Locthwain / Court of Cunning** (73/62/46%): we run Court of Ire, the one Court that is removal as well as reach; the others are drain-or-discard, theft-casting and mill engines that suit the Long Morning build better. This deck's upkeep value comes from spells (Galvanoth, Mirror, rebound).
- **Creeping Bloodsucker** (68%): an upkeep drain body; Guttersnipe gives more reach in a spell deck.
- **Plargg and Nassari** (61%): a random, symmetrical upkeep engine at 5 mana; Galvanoth is ours alone.
- **Extravagant Replication** (61%): 6 mana and copies permanents; this deck's engine is spells. Quantum Misalignment covers copying Obeka.
- **Mechanized Production** (60%): artifact-copy plan; we have few artifacts worth copying.
- **Inspiring Refrain / Rousing Refrain** (55/46%): suspend card flow; Recurring Insight and rebound draw do this now.
- **Nightscape Familiar** (52%): a cost reducer for blue/red spells; Storm-Kiln Artist does more for a spell deck.
- **Jhoira of the Ghitu** (46%): replaced by Ojer Pakpatiq.
- **Lightning Greaves** (46%): shroud stops our own auras and Sword; Swiftfoot Boots, Ring of Evos Isle and Psychic Paper instead.
- **Suspended Sentence** (45%): replaced by Maestros Charm and the rebound removal.
- **Silent Arbiter** (44%): a symmetrical one-attacker rule; we'd rather keep blockers out of the way with removal and evasion.
- **Indulgent Tormentor / Skyline Despot** (43%): five- and seven-drop creatures whose upkeep value the opponent can refuse (Tormentor) or that needs the monarch (Despot); the top end is Cast Through Time and Recurring Insight.
- **An Offer You Can't Refuse** (43%): gives opponents Treasures; Izzet Charm, Swan Song and Drown in the Loch instead.
- **Paradox Haze** (40%): adds one upkeep after our first each turn, repeating the upkeep permanents (Galvanoth, Mirror, Thassa, Theater, Court of Ire) but not rebound or Obeka's extra upkeeps; it's on the swap list.
- **Profane Tutor** (40%): suspend 2 tutor; Demonic Tutor is our one tutor.
- **Exotic Orchard / Crumbling Necropolis / Reliquary Tower** (60/53/49%): we run true duals, shocks, fetches and a triome; Thought Vessel was cut too, and hand size hasn't been a problem.

## Testing

**Goldfish (`./edh analyze`, 10,000 trials, 36 lands, average nonland MV 2.86).** Obeka's average first cast
is T3.8: 84% on curve, 91% by T5, colour-blocked 2%. Screw (2 or fewer lands on T4) is 7%; flood (9+ lands
seen by T7) is 2%. Targeted removal in hand by T2: 89%. Flood is low enough that 35 lands is possible, but the Mirror
(5 mana, then X to imprint) and holding up counters both want land drops, so I kept 36.

**Engine (`./edh engine`).**
- *Obeka turn armed* (commander + one evasion/protection piece + one doubling payoff cast): 49% by T5, **61% by T6**
  (median T5), 74% by T8. I accept 60%+ at T6 as the target: the goldfish draws one card a turn and models none
  of our ~15 selection and draw spells, so this is a floor. Bottleneck at T6: evasion not drawn 18%, payoff not
  drawn 11% or drawn but not deployed 9%, Obeka drawn-but-not-deployed (mana) 8%.
- *Obeka doubling the spellbook* (the brief's stricter two-payoff version): 17% by T5, **32% by T6**, 51% by T8.
  Bottleneck: second payoff not drawn (39%). I didn't push this further. The fixes would be more rebound filler
  (Void Squall, Sight Beyond Sight, Blessed Reincarnation), each weaker than the interaction it would replace,
  and a one-payoff Obeka turn already beats a plain attack.
- *Interaction held up* (two counters/removal in hand): 87% by T5.
- Going from the first draft to now, armed-by-T6 went from 59% to 61%. Adding Thassa and Key to the City fixed
  most of the evasion gap; the later swaps (Court of Ire for Consuming Vapors, Aether Tunnel for Distortion
  Strike) cost 3–4 points, accepted for card quality.

**Audits.**
- *Omissions:* 27 `!!` staples, each answered under Omitted staples. Two were added after the audit (Court of
  Ire, Aether Tunnel), plus Ring of Evos Isle after re-reading it (its counter comes in every upkeep).
- *Claims:* run 1 (deck only), 63 claims: 12 overstated, 1 wrong (Izzet Charm "good twice" without naming
  Ojer/CTT), 2 unclear, all fixed. Run 2 (deck + notes), 103 claims: 20 overstated, 1 wrong (calling Guttersnipe
  magecraft), 5 unclear, all fixed. Most findings were "rebound only from hand / only if cast precombat" precision
  and the Swan Song/Negate scope. Run 3 (after writing these notes), 103 claims: 10 overstated, 1 wrong
  (Indulgent Tormentor is a five-drop), 2 unclear, all fixed. Run 4, 103 claims: 97 ok, 3 overstated, 2 unclear,
  1 "wrong" (the judge read "after Fireball" as the X-spell Fireball; the note now names Delayed Blast Fireball). All
  six were rewritten.
- *Gems:* run 1 hit rate 95% (20/21 judged; 7 annotations named a staple that's also in the deck, all re-pointed).
  Run 2, 28 gems: **86% gem better (24/28)**. Of the four "staple better" findings, two were swapped (Consuming
  Vapors → Court of Ire, clear; Distortion Strike → Aether Tunnel, slight) and two are defended above (Guttersnipe,
  Combat Research).

**Forge smoke test** (`./edh sim --bracket 3 --games 16`, `sims/20261011-063149-b3`). It ran on the list *before*
the last three swaps (it had Cloak of Mists, Distortion Strike and Consuming Vapors where Ring of Evos Isle,
Aether Tunnel and Court of Ire are now). I didn't re-sim, because three one-for-one swaps are below what 16 games
resolve.
- **Won 1/16 (6%, 95% CI 1%–28%; baseline 25%)**, 1 timeout/draw, 0 errored games. Average game 10.2 rounds; we
  died on round 9.9 on average, all to life loss against creature decks (Edgar Markov, Y'shtola, Giada, Sauron).
  This is a smoke test, not a score. Forge's AI plays draw-go badly: it taps out, and it can't cast five of our
  cards (below).
- Commander cast in 100% of games, but first on **round 6.2** against a goldfish T3.8. The AI casts rocks and cheap
  spells first and holds Obeka. Opponents' removal (Swords, Path) recast her later in several games.
- **The mechanism works in Forge.** Logs show Obeka connecting and the extra upkeeps resolving. Staggershock and
  Distortion Strike were cast precombat and rebound in the *same turn's* extra upkeep (pod02, game 2). Thassa's
  scry fired once per upkeep, and Cast Through Time resolved and rebounded Staggershock (pod01).
- **Never cast (12) and verdicts:**
  - *Flagged unplayable for the AI, so the sim is blind:* Panoptic Mirror, Fire Covenant, Toxic Deluge, Recurring
    Insight. Keep all four.
  - *Additional-cost draw the AI avoids:* Unexpected Windfall, Big Score (discard as a cost). Keep; they're how
    Treasures survive into main phase 2 on Obeka turns.
  - *Situational:* Izzet Charm, Faithless Salvaging (the AI rarely loots), World at War (the AI discarded it once,
    since it doesn't plan extra combats), Into the Time Vortex (discarded once). Keep. World at War is a human-piloted
    finisher.
  - *Never seen in the logs at all (probably not drawn in 16 short games):* Guttersnipe, Theater of Horrors. No
    evidence either way.
  - None of these is a card I'd cut on re-reading. The real caveat is that the sim can't test the Mirror plan at
    all.

## Bracket

`./edh validate deck.txt --bracket 3`: **PASS**. Game Changers (3): Panoptic Mirror, Fierce Guardianship,
Demonic Tutor. No two-card combos detected; Commander Spellbook's deck tag is "Powerful (≈bracket 3+)", from
card power, not combos. No REVIEW items.

My own review questions:
- *Is World at War an extra-turn effect?* No, it's extra combats. It can give three combats in a turn, but once
  per game unless recurred (Fantastic Elasticity can regrow it). No loop exists in the deck.
- *Is Panoptic Mirror + Delayed Blast Fireball a "two-card combo"?* It's a deterministic clock (5 per upkeep),
  not an infinite. It needs 8 mana over two turns (5 + 3 to imprint) and kills over several turns, or one
  Obeka turn when the table is low. That fits bracket 3's "no early-game two-card infinites".
- *Spirit of the bracket:* three Game Changers and efficient interaction put the deck at the strong end of
  bracket 3, but it has no fast kill; the realistic win is turn 8–11.

## Swaps considered

- **Paradox Haze** for Sensei's Divining Top: a second upkeep every turn for Galvanoth, Mirror, Theater and Court of
  Ire, even when Obeka doesn't connect. Strong if you lean into the permanents.
- **Thousand-Year Storm** for Into the Time Vortex: rebound casts in the upkeeps count as later spells and get
  copied more. Huge when it works, but it's a second 6+ mana do-nothing beside Cast Through Time.
- **Night's Whisper** for Transpose (see Gems).
- **Rhystic Study** or **Mana Drain** for Demonic Tutor, if you prefer card flow or tempo to consistency (the
  Game Changer budget is full).
- **Spellbinder** (imprint an instant, cast a copy free on each Obeka hit) for Key to the City: more payoff,
  but equip {4} is heavy.
- **Veyran, Voice of Duality** for Storm-Kiln Artist: triggers caused by casting or copying an instant or
  sorcery (Archmage, Guttersnipe, Storm-Kiln) trigger an additional time; it does nothing for upkeep triggers.
- **Boltwave / Prisoner's Dilemma** for more fixed burn if your table plays slow.
- **Blessed Reincarnation / Void Squall** for more rebound density; both rejected for value (a free creature
  for the opponent; 5 mana for a bounce).
- **Distortion Strike / Consuming Vapors**: cut after the gem audit. Distortion Strike's rebound arrives after combat on
  the turns it matters (wasted), and Consuming Vapors lost the slot to Court of Ire. Either is a fine 1-for-1 back in.
- **Thought Vessel** back for Mind Stone if hand size becomes a problem.
