# Browse: cheap instant-speed interaction

Slices: instants MV<=3 with "counter target" (230 cards) and instants MV<=2 with destroy/exile/bounce/-X/3+ damage (237 cards), read in full.

## Counters
- Counterspell, Mana Drain — staples.
- Fierce Guardianship — free with Obeka out (Game Changer).
- Get Out — counter a creature/enchantment spell OR bounce two of ours (Pact exit, save Obeka). Unlisted.
- Archmage's Charm — counter / draw 2 / steal an MV<=1 permanent (a Sol Ring).
- Drown in the Loch — UB counter-or-kill; scales with graveyards, which our removal fills.
- Swan Song — 1 mana vs. the enchantment/instant/sorcery that kills our engine.
- Keep Safe — counters removal aimed at our permanents + draws; narrower than Swan Song.
- Disallow — counters triggers too; 3 mana.
- Rejected: Pact of Negation (its payment comes in our next upkeep, at 5 mana).

## Removal
- Infernal Grasp, Go for the Throat, Terminate, Bitter Triumph — creature kill that leaves the body in a graveyard for Virtue (exile would not).
- Untimely Malfunction — artifact removal, OR two creatures can't block (Obeka connects), OR redirect a spell.
- Chaos Warp — any permanent, red.
- Feed the Swarm — sorcery, but black enchantment removal.
- Alchemist's Retrieval — bounce anything (cleave) or our own Pact for {U}.
- Pongify / Rapid Hybridization — they get a 3/3 that blocks Obeka; skipped.
- Cyclonic Rift — instant one-sided mass bounce (Game Changer).
- Wipes: Toxic Deluge at 4 leaves Obeka (2/5) alive; Vile Requiem is one-sided.
