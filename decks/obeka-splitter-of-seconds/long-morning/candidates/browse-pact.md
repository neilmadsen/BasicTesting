# Browse: give away or reset Demonic Pact

Slice: regex over give/exchange control of a permanent, self-flicker, "return target (enchantment/permanent) you control" (20 cards) plus the instant-removal slice for self-bounce.

## Kill routes (Pact with 3 modes used goes to an opponent; their upkeep forces "you lose the game")
- Donate — {2}{U} sorcery. Cleanest. Main phase 2 after the Obeka turn.
- Puca's Mischief — upkeep trigger: order it to resolve after Pact's 3rd mode in the same upkeep, swap Pact for their MV<=4 nonland permanent.
- Harmless Offering — {2}{R} sorcery, second copy of Donate.
- Shifting Grift — {U}{U}+{1} spree: exchange Pact for their enchantment (needs them to have one).
- Role Reversal / Shifting Loyalties / Burglar's Plot — same, need a shared type; costlier.
- Bazaar Trader — can't: artifact/creature/land only. Fateful Handoff — artifact/creature only.

## Safe exits (instant speed; can be used in the upkeep step right after the 3rd mode resolves)
- Get Out — {U}{U}: return 1-2 of our creatures/enchantments, or counter a creature/enchantment spell.
- Alchemist's Retrieval — {U}: return our permanent; cleave {1}{U} hits anything.
- Vedalken Mastermind — {U},T: repeatable self-bounce (Pact, Court of Ire for a fresh monarch ETB, or Obeka).
- Teferi's Time Twist — {1}{U}: exile Pact, returns at next end step as a NEW object with no modes chosen.
- Shimmerwing Chimera — upkeep "up to one" bounce; same-step ordering.
- Otawara, Soaring City — channel from hand.
- Rescue — {U}; strictly worse than Alchemist's Retrieval here.

## Rejected
- Estrid's Invocation as a Pact reset — when it flickers it must re-copy an enchantment we control, so it needs the original Pact still on the battlefield, which is still counting toward mode 4. Used on Court of Ire instead.
- Blim, Comedic Genius — gives on combat damage, before the extra upkeeps; wrong timing.
- Eyes Everywhere / Avarice Totem / Conjured Currency — exchange themselves, not Pact.
