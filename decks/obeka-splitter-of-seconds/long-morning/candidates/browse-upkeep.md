# Browse: "at the beginning of your upkeep" (removal / theft / reanimation / draw)

Slices read in full: pool-upkeep-answers.md (126 cards, regex upkeep + destroy|damage|gain control|under your control|sacrifices|discards|draw|exile, drawback noise removed) and pool-upkeep-other.md (326 cards, every other "at the beginning of your upkeep" card, same noise filter).

## Taken or seriously considered
- Court of Ire — 5 — 2/7 damage any target per upkeep; ETB monarch. Staple (77%), the best card in the slice.
- Virtue of Persistence — 7 (adventure 2) — reanimate from ANY graveyard each upkeep. Unlisted on Obeka B3 page.
- Frenzied Fugue — 4 — steals any permanent each of our turns, untaps it each upkeep. Pair with a sac outlet for permanent removal.
- Parasitic Implant — 4 — slow Murder that is immediate on an Obeka turn (extra upkeep kills it).
- Demonic Pact — 4 — three good modes, then an exit. Needs planning (see browse-pact.md).
- Enslave — 6 — permanent steal + 1 damage to owner per upkeep (3 on an Obeka turn).
- Gisa, Glorious Resurrector — 4 — opponents' creatures that die are exiled, then come to us at our upkeep. Obeka's post-combat upkeeps return anything that died in combat that same turn.
- Dominus of Fealty — 5 — steal any permanent each upkeep until EOT, untapped. Three different permanents on an Obeka turn; feed them to Goblin Bombardment.
- Vengeful Strangler // Strangling Grasp — 2 — when it dies it becomes an aura: the enchanted player sacrifices a nonland permanent + loses 1 each of our upkeeps.
- Legacy's Allure — 2 — treasure counter per upkeep (3+ per Obeka turn), sac at instant speed: permanent steal of a creature with power <= counters.
- Vile Requiem — 4 — verse counter per upkeep; {1}{B} sac: destroy X nonblack creatures (Obeka is black, so it never has to hit her).
- Recantation — 5 — verse counters; {U} sac: bounce X permanents at instant speed.
- Puca's Mischief — 4 — swap one of ours for one of theirs (equal or lower MV) each upkeep. Doubles as the Pact kill route.
- Xanathar, Guild Kingpin — 6 — target opponent can't cast spells until EOT; on an Obeka turn each extra upkeep locks a different opponent for our second main phase.
- Phyrexian Arena — 3 — draw per upkeep. Staple.
- Bloodgift Demon — 5 — flier, draw per upkeep (target player: can also finish someone at 1-3 life).
- Mind Unbound — 6 — lore counters: 1+2+3 = 6 cards on the first Obeka turn.
- Paradox Haze — 3 — an extra upkeep every turn regardless of Obeka. Crowd card (40%) but the best amplifier.
- Extravagant Replication — 6 — token copy of Court of Ire / Virtue each upkeep. Crowd card (65%), best card for the slot.
- Estrid's Invocation — 3 — copy of Court of Ire that flickers each upkeep = re-crown ourselves monarch every upkeep.
- Shimmerwing Chimera — 4 — "up to one" upkeep bounce of our enchantment: Pact exit (order after the 3rd mode), Court rebuy.
- Taigam, Sidisi's Hand — 5 — skip draw, upkeep look at 3 keep 1, rest to graveyard (fuels Virtue); exile X from gy: -X/-X.
- Staff of Nin — 6 — draw + ping. Fine, slower than Arena/Bloodgift.
- Capricious Efreet — 6 — random destroy among 1 ours + 2 theirs; aiming "ours" at a spent Pact or a Myr token is cute, but random.

## Rejected (and why)
- Culling Scales — lowest MV nonland permanent among ALL players: at a B3 table that is almost always a Treasure, token or 1-mana rock (ours included: Sol Ring, Implant's Myr). Never reaches a real threat. Brief named it; cut deliberately.
- Conjured Currency — the swap hands the enchantment to an opponent, who swaps it for our best permanent on their upkeep. Puca's Mischief swaps without giving away the engine.
- Form of the Dragon — 15 damage per Obeka turn but life becomes 5 every end step; one drain/burn spell kills us.
- Anowon, Smokestack, Stormfist Crusader, Scrawling Crawler, Plague Spitter, Festering Evil, Descent into Madness — symmetric; hit us in each extra upkeep.
- Portal to Phyrexia — 9 mana; Virtue does the reanimation for 7.
- Sheoldred, Whispering One — our graveyard only; Virtue is strictly the better reanimator here.
- Sphere of Annihilation — exiles graveyard creatures (fights Virtue) and Obeka at X>=4.
- Court of Locthwain / Stolen Strategy / Nassari / Plargg and Nassari — random value from the top of libraries; brief says avoid.
- Captive Audience — works on the opponent's upkeep, so Obeka doesn't multiply it.
- Inevitable End, Necrotic Plague — trigger on the enchanted creature's controller's upkeep, not ours.
- Sagas (The Trickster-God's Heist etc.) — lore counters come in the precombat main phase; extra upkeeps do nothing.
