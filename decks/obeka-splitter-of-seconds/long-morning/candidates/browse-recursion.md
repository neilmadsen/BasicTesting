# Browse: recursion for enchantments and artifacts

Slice: regex "return ... (enchantment|artifact|permanent) card(s) from your graveyard" (25 cards) + scry for generic card recursion. Grixis is thin here.

- Crystal Chimes — {3}; {3},T,sac: all enchantment cards from graveyard to hand. Best mass refill after a Disenchant-wipe (Court, Virtue, Pact, Arena, Allure...).
- Skull of Orm — {3}; {5},T: one enchantment to hand. Repeatable but 8 mana per use.
- Codex Shredder — {1}; {5},T,sac: any card back.
- Abstergo Entertainment — land; historic card back (artifacts, legendaries: Gisa, Xanathar).
- Argivian Restoration / Reconstruction — artifacts only; our artifacts are mostly rocks.
- Virtue of Persistence covers creatures. Get Out / Alchemist's Retrieval / Vedalken Mastermind save enchantments in response to removal, which is better than recursion.
Taken: Crystal Chimes. Considered: Skull of Orm, Codex Shredder.
