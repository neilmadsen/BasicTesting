# The Long Morning — Obeka, Splitter of Seconds (Bracket 3)

Upkeep-permanent control. The board fills with permanents that remove, steal, reanimate or draw at the
beginning of our upkeep, and every time Obeka connects she adds that many more upkeeps after combat. Behind
that sit eight counterspells and a pile of instant-speed removal. The deck grinds the table's permanents
rather than racing.

## Plan

**How it wins (four routes, same engine):**
1. **Court of Ire as a cannon.** Monarch plus Court is 7 damage to any target each upkeep. Estrid's
   Invocation enters as a second Court of Ire and flickers itself each upkeep. Its re-entry makes us the
   monarch again before the damage resolves, so both Courts deal 7 even after we lost the crown. Extravagant
   Replication adds a token Court every upkeep. Two Courts on a three-upkeep turn is 42 damage to spread around.
2. **Their creatures become ours.** Gisa (their dead come back to us at our upkeep), Virtue of Persistence
   (reanimate from any graveyard every upkeep), Enslave, Legacy's Allure, Frenzied Fugue, Dominus of Fealty,
   Puca's Mischief. The table's best threats end up attacking alongside an unblockable Obeka.
3. **Demonic Pact handed over.** Fire Pact's three good modes, then Puca's Mischief swaps it to an opponent
   in the same upkeep. On their upkeep the only mode left is "you lose the game".
4. **Reach.** Court of Ambition (6 life or two cards from each opponent per upkeep as monarch), Torch Song
   and Goblin Bombardment at instant speed, Bloodgift Demon aimed at a player on low life, Pact's 4 damage.

**Key turns.**
- T1–3: rocks, Rhystic Study, Phyrexian Arena or Paradox Haze, the cheap upkeep pieces (Legacy's Allure,
  Vengeful Strangler, Torch Song). Hold up Counterspell, Swan Song or a kill spell.
- T4: Obeka. From now on Fierce Guardianship and Deadly Rollick are free, so we can tap out more safely.
- T5–7: deploy two upkeep answers **before combat**, give Obeka a way through, and connect. Every
  permanent on the battlefield when combat damage happens triggers again in each extra upkeep.
- T7+: the first long morning: two or three upkeeps of answers, draws and steals, then a second main phase
  with the table's spells locked by Xanathar or answered by held counters.

**Mulligan.** Keep 3–4 lands, or 2 lands plus a rock, with a blue source. Ideally also one piece of
interaction or one upkeep permanent. Ship 1-landers, 6–7 landers, and hands with no blue (the counters are
the glue). Don't mulligan just because Obeka is missing: the deck has 30 two-drops to bridge to her.

## Pilot notes

**Timing rules that decide games.**
- Obeka's extra upkeeps come **after combat**, before our second main phase. Pump, equip and haste don't
  benefit from them that turn. Upkeep permanents must be on the battlefield before combat damage.
- Mana empties between steps. An instant cast in an extra upkeep needs mana in that step. Plan the upkeep
  responses (Pact exits, Legacy's Allure, Vile Requiem, Torch Song, Recantation) against main-phase spending.
- **We order our own upkeep triggers.** Put Demonic Pact's trigger on top so it resolves first and the exit
  (Puca's Mischief, Shimmerwing Chimera) resolves after it. Put Estrid's flicker on top of the Court damage
  triggers so we are the monarch when they resolve.
- "Until end of turn" control (Dominus of Fealty, Frenzied Fugue) ends at **cleanup**. Sacrifice borrowed
  creatures to Goblin Bombardment, Phyrexian Altar or Diabolic Intent before then. That is permanent removal,
  and the card goes to its owner's graveyard where Virtue of Persistence can take it back for keeps.
- Our upkeep drawbacks are paid once per upkeep. Phyrexian Arena costs 3 life on a three-upkeep turn, and
  Bloodgift Demon costs another 3 if it targets us. Watch the life total; Pact's 4 life and Toxic Deluge's
  cost interact with this.
- Opponents' "each upkeep" / "each player's upkeep" permanents (Sulfuric Vortex, Smokestack, Tangle Wire) hit us in **every** extra upkeep. Remove them before a long morning.

**Demonic Pact protocol (never break this).**
1. Cast Pact only when an exit is ready: an instant (Teferi's Time Twist, Get Out, Alchemist's Retrieval,
   Vedalken Mastermind untapped, Otawara in hand, a charged Recantation) or an upkeep exit already on board
   (Shimmerwing Chimera, Puca's Mischief).
2. Count upkeeps, not turns. With Paradox Haze, Pact sees two upkeeps every turn. An Obeka hit adds more in the
   same turn, so the third mode can resolve mid-turn. Remove Pact in that same step: there is priority between
   the trigger resolving and the next upkeep starting.
3. Kill route: Puca's Mischief resolving after the third mode swaps Pact (MV 4) for an opponent's MV ≤ 4 nonland
   permanent. Reset route: Teferi's Time Twist (back at end step as a new object, no modes used), or bounce
   and recast it.
4. Never let Extravagant Replication or Estrid's Invocation copy Demonic Pact.

**Key lines.**
- *Court loop:* Court of Ire → Estrid's Invocation copying it. Each upkeep the flicker resolves first
  (monarch), then both Courts deal 7.
- *Doubling upkeeps:* Extravagant Replication copying Paradox Haze (the token aura enchants us). Each Haze adds
  an upkeep on our first upkeep of the next turn. Upkeeps per turn roughly double each turn. It's bounded, not
  infinite, but it multiplies everything, including the life cost of Arena.
- *Steal and sacrifice:* Dominus takes a different creature in each upkeep → Goblin Bombardment or
  Phyrexian Altar → Virtue later reanimates the best one permanently.
- *Board flip:* with Gisa out, Vile Requiem (Gisa is black, so it never has to target her) or Toxic Deluge **for
  3** (keeps Gisa 4/4 and Obeka 2/5 alive) → their creatures arrive under our control next upkeep.
- *Strangler:* Vengeful Strangler dies (sacrifice it to Goblin Bombardment or Diabolic Intent) → Strangling
  Grasp on their best creature → that player sacrifices a nonland permanent and loses 1 life each of our upkeeps.
- *Xanathar turn:* each extra upkeep targets a different opponent, so none of them can cast spells while we
  resolve the big second main phase.

**Hold up.** On opponents' turns keep two mana for Counterspell, Mana Drain, Get Out or a kill spell. Once Obeka
is out, Fierce Guardianship and Deadly Rollick are free, so the hold can drop to one card's worth. Hold Swan Song
for enchantment wipes.

**Answer on sight.** Enchantment wipes and mass bounce (Farewell, Austere Command, Bane of Progress, Back to
Nature): counter them. Graveyard hate that blanks Virtue (Rest in Peace, Leyline of the Void). Pillowfort that
stops Obeka attacking (Ghostly Prison, Propaganda). Symmetric upkeep punishers. Notion Thief / Opposition Agent.
Torpor Orb / Hushbringer stop the enter triggers that hand us the monarchy (Court of Ire, Court of Ambition, Estrid's
copies, Replication tokens). Remove them before relying on the Court loop.

**Never.**
- Never cast upkeep permanents in the second main phase on a turn Obeka is about to connect.
- Never let Demonic Pact see a fourth upkeep (see protocol).
- Never pay 5+ for Toxic Deluge (Obeka is 2/5), and pay 3 if Gisa is out.
- Never attack Obeka into an untapped double block without evasion. Her value is the trigger, not the damage.
- Never aim Court of Ire at a player when an opponent's creature is about to take the monarch and kill us; kill
  the creature.
- Gisa and Virtue don't stack. While Gisa is out, opponents' dying creatures are exiled, not put into a
  graveyard, so Virtue only finds what was already there.

## Key cards and lines

1. **Obeka** — every point of combat damage is another upkeep. Ring of Evos Isle grows her in every upkeep, so
   the next hit is bigger.
2. **Court of Ire + Estrid's Invocation** — retake the monarchy every upkeep; 14 damage per upkeep.
3. **Extravagant Replication** — copies Court, Virtue or Paradox Haze, and the copies compound.
4. **Virtue of Persistence** — the best creature in any graveyard, every upkeep. Early it's a 2-mana -3/-3.
5. **Gisa, Glorious Resurrector** — turns our removal and wipes into theft.
6. **Demonic Pact + Puca's Mischief** — three modes, then the swap.
7. **Dominus of Fealty + Goblin Bombardment** — three steal-and-sacrifices on a three-upkeep turn.
8. **Paradox Haze** — two upkeeps a turn without Obeka connecting.
9. **Legacy's Allure** — 2 mana that becomes an instant-speed permanent steal.

## Gems

Hit rate from `audit gems`: see Testing. "Staple better" verdicts are discussed per card.

Each is under 5% of Obeka decks on EDHREC (most are unlisted). Each line says what it does here that the staple it
displaced doesn't, followed by the blind gem-audit verdict.

- **Vengeful Strangler** (vs Creeping Bloodsucker) — once it dies, it's an edict every upkeep: three sacrifices from
  one player on a three-upkeep turn instead of three points of drain. *Gem better.*
- **Vedalken Mastermind** (vs Lightning Greaves) — a repeatable instant-speed bounce of our own permanent: the Pact
  exit, Obeka out of a removal spell, Court of Ire back for a fresh monarch trigger. Greaves' shroud would block our
  own tricks. *Gem better.*
- **Gisa, Glorious Resurrector** (vs Indulgent Tormentor) — every creature our removal or wipes kill comes back as
  ours at the next upkeep, and on an Obeka turn that's the same turn. *Labelled staple better, but the judge's own
  reasoning favours Gisa ("turns our heavy removal into theft... Tormentor lets opponents choose the least painful
  option"). Kept; I read it as a mislabel.*
- **Shimmerwing Chimera** (vs Twilight Prophet) — the only upkeep-timed "up to one" bounce of our enchantments in
  the colours. It picks up Pact right after its third mode and rebuys Court of Ire. *Gem better.*
- **Key to the City** (vs Whispersilk Cloak) — unblockable without shroud. Cloak's shroud would stop Ring of Evos
  Isle, Brotherhood Regalia, Aqueous Form, Get Out, Teferi's Time Twist and Vedalken Mastermind from targeting
  Obeka. The discard is excess lands in a deck with this much draw, and Key also draws. *Staple better (slight);
  defended on the shroud conflict, which the judge didn't weigh.*
- **Crystal Chimes** (vs Palace Siege) — the engine is enchantments; Chimes returns all of them after a Farewell.
  *Gem better.*
- **Legacy's Allure** (vs Inevitable Betrayal) — 2 mana, charged by every upkeep, then a permanent steal at
  instant speed. *First run, named against Dragonmaster Outcast: labelled staple better, but with reasoning that
  favours Allure ("Obeka grows it fast into big theft. Outcast isn't theft"). Re-pointed to a theft staple; see the re-run below.*
- **Torch Song** (vs Volcanic Geyser) — a Fireball the upkeeps pay for, at instant speed. *Gem better.*
- **Demonic Pact** (vs Court of Ambition) — 4 damage to any target (removal, not just drain), two discards, two
  cards, then reset or swap it to kill. *Staple better (slight): the judge cited the liability. Defended: the
  liability is managed by seven exits and a written protocol. In response, Court of Ambition is now in the deck as
  well, in place of Mind Unbound.*
- **Frenzied Fugue** (vs Court of Embereth) — takes the table's best permanent every turn (creature, rock or
  planeswalker). With Bombardment, it takes it for good. *Gem better.*
- **Puca's Mischief** (vs Court of Vantress) — the deck's only Pact kill, and a value swap every upkeep. *Gem better.*
- **Vile Requiem** (vs Blasphemous Act) — a one-sided, targeted, instant-speed wipe charged by the upkeeps. Act kills
  Obeka. *Gem better.*
- **Recantation** (vs Aetherize) — a scaling instant mass bounce that can include our spent Pact. *Gem better.*
- **Enslave** (vs Inevitable Betrayal) — immediate theft plus an upkeep ping. *Gem better.*
- **Alchemist's Retrieval** (vs Into the Roil) — the one-mana Pact exit, or a cleave bounce. *Gem better.*
- **Drown in the Loch** (vs Negate) — counters anything or kills a creature; the graveyards fill in long games.
  *Gem better.*
- **Get Out** (vs Arcane Denial) — a counter, or a two-card save and Pact exit. *Gem better.*
- **Teferi's Time Twist** (vs Deflecting Swat) — resets Pact as a new object and dodges removal. *Gem better.*
- Swapped after the audit: **Taigam** (lost to Bonehoard Dracosaur) and **Parasitic Implant** (lost to Deadly Rollick).
  Both staples are now in the deck.

## Omitted staples

From `audit omissions --bracket 3` (Obeka's B3 page, 40%+ inclusion). Replicating Ring and Court of Ambition
were on the list and are now in the deck.
- **As Foretold** (79%) — the suspend sub-theme. We run no suspend cards; with no time counters it does nothing.
- **Twilight Prophet** (79%) — needs ten permanents for the city's blessing and is random value. Shimmerwing
  Chimera took the 4-mana flier slot because it is a Pact exit.
- **Dragonmaster Outcast** (75%) — a 1-mana must-answer dragon factory, but it doesn't answer anything. Legacy's Allure
  is the cheap upkeep piece instead.
- **Court of Embereth** (73%) — a good clock, but it takes nothing. Frenzied Fugue took the red 4-drop slot.
- **Court of Vantress** (68%) — copies are value; Puca's Mischief is the blue 4-mana upkeep enchantment because it is
  also the Pact kill. Estrid's Invocation and Extravagant Replication already cover copying.
- **Creeping Bloodsucker** (68%) — a 1-point drain. Vengeful Strangler is the 2-drop that becomes an edict per upkeep.
- **Ring of Valkas / Ring of Xathrid** (68% / 59%) — growth on Obeka. One Ring (Evos Isle) is in, for its hexproof.
  More rings drift toward voltron; the deck wants answers, not a 9-power commander. First add if you want her bigger.
- **Court of Locthwain / Plargg and Nassari** (62% / 61%) — random value off the top of libraries. The brief rules out
  "upkeep effects that are just random value".
- **Mechanized Production** (60%) — an alternate win through copying an artifact eight times; off-plan.
- **Inspiring Refrain / Rousing Refrain** (55% / 46%) — suspend cards; same reason as As Foretold.
- **Jhoira of the Ghitu** (46%) — suspend enabler.
- **Suspended Sentence** (45%) — slow removal through suspend; we have instant removal.
- **Nightscape Familiar** (52%) — cost reducer for red/blue spells. Our expensive cards are black or colorless enchantments.
- **Silent Arbiter** (44%) — symmetric; it would cap our stolen creatures' attacks too.
- **Skyline Despot** (43%) — 7-mana value; the monarch here comes from the Courts.
- **Court of Cunning** (46%) — milling opponents feeds Virtue but doesn't answer anything; a near-miss.
- **An Offer You Can't Refuse** (43%) — a fine 1-mana counter. Swan Song does the same job without giving two Treasures.
- **Profane Tutor** (40%) — suspend tutor that Obeka speeds up; Grim Tutor and Diabolic Intent are faster without a hit.
- **Reliquary Tower** (49%) — the draw is steady, not explosive, since Mind Unbound was cut. We spend cards as we draw
  them. A colourless land costs more than the occasional discard.
- **Xander's Lounge / Crumbling Necropolis / Stormcarved Coast** (56% / 53% / 42%) — tapped lands. With proxies the
  original duals, shocks, fetches and fast lands are strictly better.
- **Lightning Greaves / Whispersilk Cloak** (46% / 41%) — shroud stops our own Ring, Regalia, Aqueous Form, Get Out,
  Teferi's Time Twist and Vedalken Mastermind from targeting Obeka. Swiftfoot Boots, Brotherhood Regalia and Key to the
  City do the jobs without shroud.
- **Blasphemous Act** (46%) — kills Obeka. Toxic Deluge (pay 4 or less) and Vile Requiem spare her.
- **Indulgent Tormentor** (43%) — opponents pick the least painful option. Gisa took the slot.

## Bracket

`./edh validate deck.txt --bracket 3` → **PASS.** No errors, no violations, no REVIEW items.
Game Changers 3/3: Rhystic Study, Cyclonic Rift, Fierce Guardianship. Commander Spellbook estimate: "Powerful
(≈bracket 3+)", from no specific combo. No mass land denial, no extra turns.

Questions the validator didn't raise but should be answered:
- **Demonic Pact + Donate was a FAIL.** The first draft had Donate and Harmless Offering. Spellbook knows Pact +
  Donate and the validator classes any two-card kill at total MV ≤ 7 as early-game, so both were cut.
- **Demonic Pact + Puca's Mischief is the same kind of kill, and Spellbook doesn't know it.** My answer: it stays,
  and it's B3-appropriate. It kills one opponent, not the table, and it isn't infinite. It needs 8 mana of permanents,
  and Pact has to sit on the battlefield for three of our upkeeps, visible and answerable, before Puca's can swap
  it. Realistic earliest kill: Pact on turn 6 with Puca's already out, then Paradox Haze plus an Obeka hit on turn 7.
  More typically turn 8+. If your table treats it as off-limits, swap Puca's Mischief for Court of Vantress.
- **Extravagant Replication + Paradox Haze** roughly doubles our upkeeps each turn. It's bounded (no loop within a
  turn), so it isn't an infinite or an extra-turn effect, but it is the deck's most explosive line. It needs two
  6- and 3-mana enchantments to survive a turn cycle.
- **Court of Ire + Estrid's Invocation** — 14 damage per upkeep is a real clock (two players on a three-upkeep
  turn), and it's deterministic. It arrives with 8 mana of enchantments and an Obeka hit, around turn 7–9.
- **Spirit of the bracket.** Two tutors (Grim Tutor, Diabolic Intent) and a lot of free and cheap interaction push
  the deck toward the strong end of B3. The kills come late and on board, so it plays like a B3 control deck,
  not a B4 combo deck.

## Swaps considered

Near-misses and flex slots, with the reason each is out:
- **Mind Unbound** — cut after review. Its draw is mandatory and its counters persist, so total draws after n upkeeps
  are n(n+1)/2. With Paradox Haze and one or two Obeka hits that's 36–55 cards by the fourth turn, and it decks us. It's
  a great card in a deck without Haze.
- **Culling Scales** — the brief's pick; cut. At a B3 table its forced target is a Treasure, token or Sol Ring, often ours.
- **Conjured Currency** — cut. After one swap the opponent owns the engine and swaps for our best permanent.
- **Donate / Harmless Offering** — cut for the bracket (see Bracket). If your table accepts a Pact kill around turn 6,
  Donate is the first card back in, over Phyrexian Altar.
- **Taigam, Sidisi's Hand** and **Parasitic Implant** — in the first draft. The gem audit preferred Bonehoard Dracosaur and
  Deadly Rollick, and on re-reading I agreed: Taigam's selection costs a draw step, and Implant is one-shot removal that
  the engine doesn't multiply.
- **Form of the Dragon** — 5 damage per upkeep (15 on an Obeka turn) and pillowfort against ground creatures, but life 5
  at every end step loses to one drain spell. It's a coin flip, not control.
- **Staff of Nin**, **Seymour Flux** — steady upkeep draw; slower than Arena and Bloodgift Demon.
- **Strionic Resonator** — copies a 7-damage Court trigger once a turn; cut for Thassa when evasion was the engine bottleneck.
- **Sphinx of the Second Sun** — an extra beginning phase every turn is enormous here, but it costs 8.
- **Capricious Efreet** — aim its "our permanent" at a spent Pact and two thirds of its destroys hit opponents. Random, though.
- **Biting Tether**, **Fatal Attraction** — more upkeep answers if you want the engine denser; both weaker than what's in.
- **Access Tunnel** — Obeka has 2 power, so it's a cheaper Rogue's Passage until the Ring grows her. A 37th land if you
  find the deck short of evasion.
- **Ring of Xathrid**, **Criminal Past** — make Obeka bigger, which means more upkeeps; first adds for a more aggressive build.
- **Bitter Triumph**, **Keep Safe**, **Disallow** — more cheap interaction; the deck already holds plenty.
- **Shifting Grift / Role Reversal** — cheap Pact give-aways. Both would put a cheap two-card kill back in, the same
  bracket problem as Donate.
