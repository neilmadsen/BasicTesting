# Vivi Ornitier: spellslinger (EDHREC bracket 3 average)

The crowd's list, used as a test subject for the pilot. The only edit is Curiosity → Resonating Lute, to
stay inside bracket 3. This brief exists so the pilot has a plan; it isn't a brew.

## How the deck works

- **Vivi Ornitier** (3 mana, 0/3) is the engine. Every noncreature spell we cast gives her a +1/+1 counter
  and pings each opponent for 1.
- **Her mana ability** (`{0}`: add X mana in U/R, where X is her power) works once per turn and only on our
  turn. Grow her first with cheap spells in the same turn, then activate her for the biggest amount, then
  spend it on the turn's big spell.
- **Doublers.** Veyran, Voice of Duality and Harmonic Prodigy make her trigger twice.
- **Damage payoffs:**
  - Guttersnipe (2 to each opponent per instant or sorcery);
  - Coruscation Mage;
  - Fiery Inscription.
- **Card engines:**
  - Archmage Emeritus, Stormcatch Mentor, Tandem Lookout, Ophidian Eye;
  - Niv-Mizzet, Parun and Niv-Mizzet, Visionary;
  - Rhystic Study, Mystic Remora.
- **Mana:** rocks, Storm-Kiln Artist's treasures, Wizard's Staff, and Vivi herself.
- **Free and cheap spells:** Frantic Search, Snap, Mana Sculpt and Gitaxian Probe count as spells for Vivi
  and cost little or nothing.

## Game plan

1. **Turns 1–3:** land, rock or signet, cantrip. Cast Vivi on turn 3, or turn 2 off Sol Ring.
2. **Protect Vivi above everything.** Equip Lightning Greaves or Swiftfoot Boots before she needs it. Keep
   up a counter (Counterspell, Arcane Denial, Negate, Swan Song, An Offer You Can't Refuse) or Slip Out the
   Back / Magic Damper when an opponent has open mana and removal colours. She costs 2 more each recast.
3. **Develop an engine next to her:** a card-draw creature, Guttersnipe or Veyran. Chain cheap spells on our
   turn to grow her before tapping her for mana.
4. **Interaction on threats that beat us:**
   - spot removal: Lightning Bolt, Abrade, Fire Magic, Pongify, Chaos Warp;
   - sweepers: Blasphemous Act (we have few creatures), Vandalblast, Cyclonic Rift (overloaded at the end
     of an opponent's turn, or the turn we win).
5. **Win by burn.** Pings from Vivi and Guttersnipe add up. Finish with:
   - an X spell paid with Vivi's mana (Crackle with Power, Jeska's Will);
   - Grapeshot after a chain;
   - Mizzix's Mastery or Chandra's Ignition on a big turn.
   Late, Niv-Mizzet, Parun with Ophidian Eye, Tandem Lookout or Niv-Mizzet, Visionary draws and pings
   without end: a legal bracket 3 late-game win.

## Priorities and traps

- **Aim burn at the player about to win,** not the lowest life total.
- **Our life is a resource,** but we have almost no blockers. Go-wide aggro, such as vampires, dinosaurs or
  elves, kills us if we ignore it. Blasphemous Act and Rift are our answers to a wide board. Don't fire them
  early for one creature.
- **Hold instant-speed interaction** on opponents' turns when Vivi is on the battlefield and exposed.
- **Don't discard or tap out** for a marginal spell on a turn when Vivi can't be protected.
