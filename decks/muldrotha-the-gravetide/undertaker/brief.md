# Muldrotha, the Gravetide: Undertaker's Toolbox (Bracket 3)

Commander: Muldrotha, the Gravetide ({3}{B}{G}{U}, 6/6). "During each of your turns, you may play a land and cast a permanent spell of each permanent type from your graveyard."

Thesis: A recurring-removal control deck. We fill the artifact, enchantment and planeswalker lanes with permanents that sacrifice themselves for an effect, so each one becomes a spell we recast every turn. Examples: Executioner's Capsule kills a creature; Seal of Primordium or Seal of Doom kills a permanent; Cryogen Relic draws on the way in and on the way out; Aether Spellbomb bounces or draws; Chromatic Star or Sphere cantrips for free; a planeswalker whose minus ability removes something and kills it. With Muldrotha on board, each opponent loses a threat every turn while we lose nothing. The creature lane handles ETB-removal creatures (Plaguecrafter, Ravenous Chupacabra-style) and value.

Win conditions: Control, then close. We grind the table down, then win with Muldrotha plus a few resilient threats. Recurring drain helps close: Gray Merchant, Syr Konrad, or Disciple of the Vault turning every sacrificed artifact into life loss. The target is to lock up the board by turns 7–9 and close within 3–4 turns after that. The deck must be able to close. "Value forever" loses to timeouts and to people conceding out of boredom, which is worse.

Engine:
- Artifact lane: sacrifice-for-effect artifacts (Executioner's Capsule, Cryogen Relic, Aether Spellbomb, Dross Skullbomb, Chromatic Star/Sphere, Conjurer's Bauble, Mishra's Bauble, Lotus Petal-style mana, Commander's Sphere, Scrabbling Claws), plus artifacts that pay off artifacts dying.
- Enchantment lane: Seals (Primordium, Removal, Doom), Kaya's Ghostform, Animate Dead-style auras, self-sacrificing enchantments.
- Planeswalker lane: walkers whose minus kills something or draws a lot, and which we're happy to fire and lose (Vraska the Unseen / Vraska Golgari Queen, Ob Nixilis Reignited, Garruk Apex Predator, Liliana variants, Tamiyo variants). Two to four only, since they're expensive.
- Sacrifice outlets that take artifacts or enchantments on demand, so "sacrifice this" permanents can fire at instant speed or twice. Payoffs for artifacts or enchantments going to the graveyard.
- Self-mill and discard to stock the lanes early (Stitcher's Supplier, Satyr Wayfinder, Eccentric Farmer, Tireless Tracker plus clues).

Wants: Any permanent with "sacrifice this: <removal or card draw>". Permanents that draw on entering or leaving the battlefield. Cheap artifacts that replace themselves. Cards that put permanents from the library into the graveyard. Instant-speed interaction for the turns before Muldrotha.

Avoid: Graveyard hate on ourselves, and exile-based self-mill. Expensive permanents with no repeatable effect. Win-more cards. Pure stax. Symmetrical taxes that hurt a deck that casts many small permanents every turn.

Interaction plan: This deck is the interaction. Aim for 12–16 removal effects counting recursive ones; 1–2 board wipes, since recurring spot removal usually beats wipes here; some counterspells or protection for Muldrotha, who is the lynchpin (Swan Song, Mystic Snake, Heroic Intervention, Lightning Greaves-style).

Bracket constraints: Bracket 3. At most 3 Game Changers; candidates are Rhystic Study, Demonic Tutor, Mystical Tutor, Bolas's Citadel and The One Ring. Spend them where the plan benefits most. No two-card early infinite combos, no mass land denial, and no lock pieces that stop opponents from playing the game at all.

Targets: 36–37 lands, ramp ~10, card draw ~10 (count recurring cantrip permanents), removal 12–16, wipes 1–2, protection 3–4.

User preferences: none given (demo run). Proxies, so no budget limit.
