# Muldrotha, the Gravetide: Undertaker's Toolbox (Bracket 3)

## Plan

This is a recurring-removal control deck. Nearly every nonland permanent either
sacrifices itself for an effect or dies on its own: Seals, Capsules, sagas,
evoke creatures, planeswalkers we minus to death, and a battle. Once Muldrotha is
out, each lane (artifact, enchantment, creature, planeswalker, battle, land)
recasts one of those from the graveyard every turn. In practice the table loses
two or three permanents per turn cycle while we lose nothing.

- **Turns 1–4:** ramp (Birds, Sakura-Tribe Elder, Signet, Talisman, Three Visits,
  Nature's Lore, Farseek), cheap card flow (Mishra's Bauble, Cryogen Relic, Mind
  Stone), and cheap removal held for real threats (Executioner's Capsule, Sinister
  Concoction, Seal of Doom, Assassin's Trophy). Casting removal from hand before
  Muldrotha is fine: it goes to the graveyard, where the lanes will want it.
  Survival of the Fittest early is the best turn-2 play: pitch whatever creature
  you'd like to recast later, and fetch the one you need now.
- **Turns 5–7: Muldrotha.** Keep Swan Song, Counterspell, Siren Stormtamer or
  Heroic Intervention up if you can. She is the lynchpin, and Kaya's Ghostform on
  her is the best use of the Ghostform. Against a wide board, Toxic Deluge first
  (before her, or at X=5 after her) buys the time.
- **Every turn after that:** land lane (a fetch, Bojuka Bog, Cabal Pit, a
  horizon land); artifact lane (Capsule for a kill, Ratchet Bomb to clear tokens,
  Wayfarer's Bauble for a land, Dalek Drone or Walking Ballista); enchantment lane
  (Seal of Doom, Sinister Concoction, Twisted Embrace, a saga); creature lane
  (Shriekmaw or Mulldrifter evoked, Sheoldred, Massacre Wurm, Gray Merchant,
  Kokusho); planeswalker lane (Grist, Dellian Fel, Vraska minus and sacrificed);
  battle lane (Invasion of Innistrad). Doc Aurlock (graveyard casts cost 2 less)
  and Gravebreaker Lamia (1 less) roughly double what the lanes can do per turn.
- **Closing (target: board locked by turns 7–9, dead table 3–4 turns later).**
  Once the board is under control, stop spending the creature lane on value and
  point it at the drain: Kokusho (sacrifice it with Claws of Gix, 5 to each
  opponent, recast next turn), Gray Merchant, Vrock (3 to each opponent at our end
  step), Massacre Wurm, Syr Konrad and Marionette Apprentice pinging off the churn,
  The Coming of Galactus (drain 2 twice, then a 16/16 flier), Walking Ballista as
  reach, and Muldrotha herself into an empty board. Don't durdle.

**Mulligan for:** 3–4 lands with at least two colors, plus one ramp piece or
one cheap interaction. Card flow is dense enough that a slow hand with lands
keeps; a no-ramp, no-interaction hand of five-drops does not.

**The Claws of Gix trick (the engine's hidden piece).** Sagas, planeswalkers and
battles don't go to the graveyard on their own schedule. Claws of Gix ({1},
sacrifice any permanent) sends them there as soon as they have done their job:
- The Death of Gwen Stacy / Binding the Old Gods: resolve chapter I (kill), then
  sacrifice. Recast next turn and it's a 3–4 mana Murder or Vindicate every turn.
- Professor Dellian Fel / Grist / Vraska: minus to kill, sacrifice the husk,
  recast next turn at full loyalty.
- Invasion of Innistrad: -13/-13 on entry, sacrifice the battle, and it is back
  next turn. (The battle lane is otherwise a one-shot, since the opponent
  protecting it rarely lets it die.)
- Kokusho: sacrifice it for 5 to each opponent, recast it next turn.
- Twisted Embrace: enchant a Mishra's Bauble / Capsule / Wayfarer's Bauble that
  will sacrifice itself anyway, and the aura drops into the graveyard for next
  turn.
Vraska's +2, Braids, Baba Lysaga and Barrin are the backup outlets.

## Piloting notes

- **Track the lanes with dice.** Put one die per lane (land, artifact,
  creature, enchantment, planeswalker, battle) next to Muldrotha and flip each as
  you use it. You will forget which lane you've spent by the third graveyard cast.
- **Artifact creatures and Grist can use either lane.** Dalek Drone, Baleful
  Strix and Walking Ballista can go through the artifact *or* the creature lane
  (you choose as you cast). Grist is a creature card in the graveyard, so it can
  use the creature *or* the planeswalker lane. Use this to double up when one lane
  is already spoken for.
- **End-step ordering: Vrock and Braids.** Vrock's trigger checks whether a
  permanent of yours left *this turn* as the end step begins. Braids' sacrifice
  happens *in* the end step, which is too late to turn Vrock on. Make sure
  something has left by main phase 2 (a Capsule activation, an evoke creature,
  a Claws of Gix sacrifice, a fetch). Order your end-step triggers so Braids'
  draw or drain resolves before any Vrock-dependent decisions.
- **The Coming of Galactus destroys a land each time Galactus attacks.** The
  trigger is mandatory and targets *any* land. Point it at a land of ours that
  Muldrotha can replay (Cabal Pit, Bojuka Bog, a horizon land) unless the table
  has agreed that land destruction is fine. Repeatedly blowing up opponents'
  lands is land denial in spirit at B3.
- **Ratchet Bomb at 0 counters also hits our own MV-0 permanents:** Claws of Gix,
  Mishra's Bauble, Walking Ballista (on the battlefield) and our tokens. Before you
  pop it, use Ballista's counters and sacrifice Claws or the Bauble for value
  in response. Or tick it to 1 to hit dorks, Sol Rings and Signets instead.
- **Don't loop The One Ring with Claws of Gix.** Resetting the burden once is
  fine. Recasting it every turn gives us protection from everything every turn,
  and with Glen Elendra and Siren Stormtamer up that plays like a prison deck,
  not a B3 one.
- **Invasion of Innistrad has flash, but only from hand.** Muldrotha's graveyard
  casts happen on our turn only.

## Key cards and lines

1. **Muldrotha + Executioner's Capsule + Seal of Doom**: two creature kills a
   turn for 3 mana with Doc Aurlock out. This is the baseline loop.
2. **Claws of Gix + saga, walker or battle**: see above. It turns sorcery-speed
   one-shots into per-turn removal.
3. **Sheoldred (MOM) in the creature lane**: each opponent sacrifices a nontoken
   creature or planeswalker every time she is recast. Barrin can bounce her to
   hand to double up.
4. **Massacre Wurm / Ratchet Bomb**: the answers to go-wide. The Wurm is a
   one-sided -2/-2 sweep (and 2 life per creature killed) from the creature lane;
   Ratchet Bomb at 0 clears every token from the artifact lane. Both can be recast
   every turn.
5. **Kokusho + Claws of Gix**: 5 to each opponent per loop, 15 damage a turn
   cycle. Vrock adds 3 each on top.
6. **Survival of the Fittest**: discard the creature you want in the graveyard
   (Muldrotha casts it), fetch the one you need in hand. Each activation is worth
   two creatures.
7. **Baba Lysaga**: tap and sacrifice a spent land plus an artifact creature
   (Baleful Strix, Dalek Drone, Walking Ballista): three card types means draw 3
   and drain 3, and Muldrotha replays both next turn.
8. **Secrets of the Dead / The One Ring / Mulldrifter**: the card engines that
   keep a removal deck from running out of gas.

## Gems (non-staples, and why each beats the obvious card)

Staples stay where they are the best card: Seal of Primordium, Seal of Doom,
Executioner's Capsule, Kaya's Ghostform, Pernicious Deed, Shriekmaw, Mulldrifter,
Sakura-Tribe Elder, Eternal Witness, Doc Aurlock, Gravebreaker Lamia, Gray
Merchant, Syr Konrad, Secrets of the Dead and Toxic Deluge are all on EDHREC's
Muldrotha page and all earn their slots here. The picks below appear on under ~5%
of Muldrotha decks, or not at all.

| Card | Lane | Why it beats the staple it replaces |
|---|---|---|
| **Claws of Gix** | artifact | The only 0-mana outlet that sacrifices *any* permanent at instant speed. Without it, sagas, walkers and battles are one-shots; with it, each is removal every turn. Ashnod's Altar and Viscera Seer only take creatures. |
| **Sinister Concoction** | enchantment | B to cast, B to kill *any* creature (Seal of Doom can't touch black). Its costs mill a card and discard one, and in this deck both are fuel. |
| **Twisted Embrace** | enchantment | Unconditional creature *or planeswalker* kill. Enchant a Bauble or Capsule that sacrifices itself anyway, and the aura drops into the graveyard for next turn. Recurring removal that Seal of Doom's "nonblack" clause can't match. |
| **The Death of Gwen Stacy** | enchantment | A 3-mana saga Murder with no color restriction; chapter II is a discard we want, chapter III is graveyard hate. With Claws it is a Murder every turn. |
| **The Coming of Galactus** | enchantment | Removal and a finisher in one slot: kill a nonland permanent, drain every opponent 2 twice, then a 16/16 flying trampler (mind its land-destruction attack trigger, see Piloting notes). |
| **Underhanded Designs** | enchantment | Kills any creature (needs 2 artifacts, which we always have), and every artifact we recast pings the table for {1}. Replaces a Seal of Removal that only bounces. |
| **Invasion of Innistrad** | battle | Opens the battle lane nobody uses: a flash -13/-13 that kills anything regardless of indestructible, and with Claws it's recastable every turn. |
| **Sheoldred (MOM front face)** | creature | Each *opponent* sacrifices a nontoken creature or planeswalker on entry: a three-for-one every time the creature lane recasts it. Plaguecrafter hits us too; Ravenous Chupacabra kills one creature. |
| **Massacre Wurm** | creature | A one-sided -2/-2 sweep that the creature lane can recast every turn, plus 2 life per opposing creature that dies. Answers the go-wide decks (elves, vampires, dragons, orc armies) that grind a spot-removal deck down. |
| **Ratchet Bomb** | artifact | At 0 counters, a sweep of every token on the table that the artifact lane rearms every turn. It does what Engineered Explosives does, but it's playable at any time and costs a flat 2. |
| **Walking Ballista** | artifact/creature | Either lane can recast it. It pings X/1 tokens, mana dorks and walkers, soaks mana late, and shoots players to finish. Aether Spellbomb only bounces or cantrips. |
| **Dalek Drone** | artifact/creature | An artifact creature, so either lane can recast it. Kills a creature, drains 3, leaves a flying menace body, and counts as two of Baba's three types. Beats Ravenous Chupacabra for one more mana. |
| **Vrock** | creature | "If a permanent you controlled left the battlefield this turn, each opponent loses 3" is almost always true in this deck. That's 9 damage per turn cycle for 5 mana. |
| **Professor Dellian Fel** | planeswalker | 4 mana, enters at 5: -3 Murder, then a 0: draw engine. Cheaper than Ob Nixilis Reignited or Vraska the Unseen, and fine to sacrifice and recast. |
| **Baba Lysaga, Night Witch** | creature | Tap and sacrifice up to three permanents; three card types means draw 3 and drain 3. A land plus an artifact creature does it, and Muldrotha replays both. |
| **Marionette Apprentice** | creature | Each opponent (not *target* opponent, as on Disciple of the Vault) loses 1 whenever an artifact or creature of ours hits the graveyard. Baubles, Capsule, evoke creatures and Treasures make it a steady clock. |
| **Barrin, Master Wizard** | creature | A sacrifice outlet for any permanent that also bounces a creature, or bounces our own Sheoldred or Shriekmaw to hand to double its ETB. |
| **Cabal Pit** | land | Removal in the land lane: at threshold, sacrifice it for -2/-2, then replay it from the graveyard. Kills mana dorks and tokens at no spell cost. |

## Bracket

`./edh validate deck.txt --bracket 3`: **PASS**. No errors, no violations, no
REVIEW items.
- Game Changers (3/3): **The One Ring**, **Survival of the Fittest**, **Demonic
  Tutor**. One card engine plus two tutors. Survival is exceptional here
  because the discarded creature is not lost (Muldrotha casts it). Rhystic Study
  was cut in the critique round: the deck already had enough draw.
- Commander Spellbook combos in the 99: none. Its "Powerful" tag reflects
  individual card strength, not a combo line.
- No mass land denial, no extra turns. Strip Mine / Ghost Quarter were left out
  deliberately: Muldrotha replaying a land destroyer every turn is MLD in
  practice. For the same reason, aim Galactus's attack trigger at our own
  replayable lands.
- **Spirit of the bracket.** This is a strong B3 deck, not a B4 one. It has no
  infinite and no fast win; it wins around turn 10–13 by attrition and drain.
  **Pre-game disclosure:** tell the pod that Massacre Wurm gets recast every
  turn (a recurring one-sided -2/-2 sweep) and that Ratchet Bomb recurs as a
  token sweep. Both cost real mana every turn and leave big creatures,
  planeswalkers and noncreature plans alone, but a token deck will feel locked.
  Don't loop The One Ring with Claws of Gix (see Piloting notes).
- Wipes: 5 (Pernicious Deed, Toxic Deluge, Massacre Wurm, Ratchet Bomb, and
  Walking Ballista as a partial one against X/1s).

## Critique round (v2 → v3)

A deck-critic review, checked against the card text, changed six slots:
- **−Invasion of Fiora, +Invasion of Innistrad.** My v2 notes called Fiora
  "near-one-sided". That was wrong. Its nonlegendary mode kills 18 of our 24
  creatures, every drain finisher among them. Looped with Claws of Gix, it's
  also a creature lock that doesn't belong at B3. Innistrad keeps the battle lane
  as clean spot removal.
- **−Stitcher's Supplier, +Kokusho, the Evening Star.** The deck was slow to
  close; the v1 sim wins came on round 13. The lanes fill themselves, so
  dedicated self-mill was the least-needed slot. Kokusho looped with Claws is 5
  to each opponent per turn.
- **−Aether Spellbomb, +Walking Ballista.** Ballista uses either lane, handles
  X/1s and walkers, and finishes games.
- **−Chromatic Star, +Ratchet Bomb.** The artifact lane had too many cantrips and
  too few answers to wide boards; the v1 losses were to go-wide decks.
- **−Rhystic Study, +Survival of the Fittest.** Two Game Changers on card draw
  was one too many with the draw already in the list. I had passed on Survival
  because Forge plays it badly, which was inconsistent with keeping Claws of Gix
  despite the same problem. Cards are picked for the table, not the sim.
- Piloting notes added (lane dice, dual-lane cards, Vrock/Braids end-step order,
  Galactus land trigger, Ratchet Bomb self-hits, no Ring loop) and a pre-game
  disclosure for Massacre Wurm recursion. Mana unchanged.

## Swaps considered (flex slots and near-misses)

**Close to the list:**
- *Rhystic Study* (Game Changer): back in over Demonic Tutor if your pod never
  pays the 1.
- *Invasion of Amonkhet*: a softer battle-lane option (card advantage, fills the
  graveyard) if the pod finds recurring removal too much.
- *Enigmatic Incarnation*: at end step, sacrifice an enchantment to put a creature
  with MV+1 onto the battlefield (Seal of Doom → Ravenous Chupacabra-class, Twisted
  Embrace → Sheoldred). A powerful toolbox engine, but it does nothing on its own.
- *Necron Deathmark*: a flash artifact creature that kills a creature and mills
  three. Two lanes, like Dalek Drone.
- *Lotuslight Dancers*: tutors a black, a green and a blue card straight into
  the graveyard. With Muldrotha that's three tutors; cut for curve.
- *Infernal Tribute*: {2}, sacrifice a nontoken permanent: draw. A second
  universal outlet with card draw, if Claws of Gix proves too fragile.
- *Chromatic Star*, *Aether Spellbomb*, *Stitcher's Supplier*: cut in the critique
  round; all fine, all low-impact.
- *Urza's Saga*: land-lane tutor for Claws of Gix, Sol Ring or the Baubles. Cut
  because a colorless, self-sacrificing land cost too much consistency in a
  three-color deck.
- *Engineered Explosives*: Ratchet Bomb does the recurring token-sweep job with
  less sunburst fuss.
- *Mystic Remora*: in v1, cut after the first sim (Forge pays its upkeep forever;
  in real play it's fine but off-plan).
- *Bolas's Citadel* (Game Changer): an alternative closer, but its life cost
  stacks badly with fetches and Cabal Pit.

**Rejected on reading:**
- *Invasion of Fiora*: see Critique round; symmetric in practice.
- *Grave Peril*: triggers on *any* nonblack creature entering, ours included.
- *Brittle Effigy*: exiles itself, so it never reaches the graveyard.
- *Nevinyrral's Disk*: kills Muldrotha too; Pernicious Deed with X=5 does the job
  with control over the number.
- *Vile Requiem*, *Deadly Designs*: too slow to charge.
- *Icy Prison*: the exiled creature returns when it leaves, which is exactly when
  we want to recycle it.
- *Pestilence*: kills our own small creatures and needs creatures to stay.
- *Plague Boiler*: destroys all nonland permanents, including our engine.
- *Anchovy & Banana Pizza*: 4 mana to kill a creature, plus 2 more to sacrifice it.
  Dalek Drone does more for 5.
- *Strip Mine / Ghost Quarter / Tectonic Edge loops*: MLD in practice at B3.
