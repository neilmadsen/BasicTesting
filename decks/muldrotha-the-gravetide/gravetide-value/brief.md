# Muldrotha, the Gravetide: Gravetide Value (Bracket 3)

Commander: Muldrotha, the Gravetide ({3}{B}{G}{U}, 6/6). "During each of your turns, you may play a land and cast a permanent spell of each permanent type from your graveyard."

Thesis: The best version of the popular plan, built as the benchmark. Mill ourselves aggressively, get value from ETB and death-trigger creatures we recast from the graveyard, reanimate a few premium threats, and grind every opponent out of resources. Creatures are the main lane, supported by staple recursion (Eternal Witness, Kaya's Ghostform, Animate Dead) and a handful of enchantments and artifacts.

Win conditions: A few premium reanimation or recast targets that end games at B3 without being two-card combos: big ETB or attack-trigger creatures, Gray Merchant-style drains, Syr Konrad with heavy milling. A combo that ends the game around turn 7+ with three or more pieces is acceptable if it happens incidentally. The target is to dominate the board by turns 6–8.

Engine:
- Self-mill: Stitcher's Supplier, Satyr Wayfinder, Grisly Salvage, surveil lands, Underrealm Lich-style draw replacement, The Gitrog Monster, Sidisi.
- Recursion: Muldrotha plus Eternal Witness, Kaya's Ghostform, Animate Dead, Necromancy, Reanimate, Gravebreaker Lamia (tutor to graveyard).
- ETB and death value creatures: Spore Frog (fog lock with Muldrotha), Plaguecrafter, Mulldrifter, Baleful Strix, Siren Stormtamer, Ravenous Chupacabra, River Kelpie, Accursed Marauder.
- Entomb or Buried Alive at most as setup. No turn-2 reanimation into game-ending fatties: that's bracket 4 feel.

Wants: Creatures whose ETB or death does something every time; graveyard payoffs (Syr Konrad, Kheru Goldkeeper-style); self-mill that also ramps or draws; reanimation targets that are value engines rather than instant wins.

Avoid: Exile-based self-mill. Self-graveyard hate. Turn-2 Archon of Cruelty or Griselbrand reanimation lines (the deck should feel upgraded, not optimized). Two-card infinite combos.

Interaction plan: ETB removal creatures as the recurring core, Seal of Primordium and Seal of Removal, 2–3 wipes (Toxic Deluge, Pernicious Deed), a couple of counterspells, graveyard protection or redundancy against Bojuka Bog-style hate.

Bracket constraints: Bracket 3. At most 3 Game Changers; candidates are Survival of the Fittest, Natural Order, Demonic Tutor, Rhystic Study and Mystical Tutor. No two-card early infinite combos, no mass land denial.

Targets: 36–37 lands, ramp ~10, card draw ~9, removal ~10, wipes 2–3, protection 2–3.

User preferences: none given (demo run). Proxies, so no budget limit.
