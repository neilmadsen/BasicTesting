# Muldrotha, the Gravetide: Gravetide Value (Bracket 3)

The benchmark build: the crowd's self-mill, ETB-creature and reanimation plan, built
with a proxy mana base and with non-staples only where they beat the staple they
replace. List: `deck.txt`. Print list: `proxies.txt`.

## Plan

**How it wins.** Fill the graveyard early. Land Muldrotha on turn 5 or 6. From then
on, each turn replays one land, one creature, one enchantment, one artifact and one
planeswalker from the yard. The recurring pieces are removal (Chupacabra, Shriekmaw
evoke, Skyfisher Spider, Grist), card flow (Mulldrifter evoke, Mystic Remora, Emeritus
of Ideation's Ancestral Recall) and tutoring (Emeritus of Woe's Demonic Tutor). The
table runs out of answers before we run out of threats. The deck closes with big
bodies and drains:

1. **Fatties off the yard.** Reanimate, Animate Dead, Necromancy, Bind to Life,
   Seedship Broodtender and Liliana put Grave Titan, Colossal Grave-Reaver, Doom
   Whisperer or Dreadhound into play by turns 4–6. After that, Muldrotha recasts
   whichever one gets killed.
2. **Death drains.** Syr Konrad and Dreadhound each drain every opponent for every
   creature that dies or is milled. Massacre Wurm (one-sided -2/-2, 2 life per
   dead creature) and Night Incarnate (evoke from the yard for a recurring -3/-3)
   turn an opponent's token board into lethal drain damage.
3. **Living Death** with a stocked yard, as a reset that we win.

**Key turns.** T1–2: a ramp one-drop (Birds, Studious First-Year, Sol Ring) or a
mill piece (Stitcher's Supplier, Seedship Broodtender, Satyr Wayfinder, Aftermath
Analyst). T3–4: an engine or value creature (Twilight Diviner, Doc Aurlock,
Blossoming Tortoise, Icetill Explorer, Emeritus of Woe tutoring the missing piece),
or an early reanimation of whatever got milled. T5–6: Muldrotha, ideally with
Lightning Greaves or Siren Stormtamer up. Hold back a Spore Frog in the yard as the
standing fog.

**Mulligan.** Keep 3–4 lands with any 1–2 mana play. Keep 2 lands plus a mana
one-drop or Sol Ring. Ship 1-landers, and ship 5+ land hands with no mill or ramp
piece. The deck doesn't need a specific card in the opener, because Emeritus of
Woe, Survival and Gravebreaker Lamia find it later.

## Key cards and lines

- **Muldrotha + Emeritus of Woe.** Each recast enters prepared, so each recast
  lets us cast Demonic Tutor for {1}{B}. With Twilight Diviner out, the token copy
  also enters prepared: two tutors from one cast.
- **Muldrotha + Doc Aurlock (+ Gravebreaker Lamia).** Yard casts cost 2 (3) less.
  Evoke Mulldrifter costs {U}, Overlord of the Balemurk's impending costs {B}, and
  Chupacabra costs {B}{B}.
- **Muldrotha + Overlords (enchantment lane).** Cast from the yard for the impending
  cost. Balemurk: mill 4 and regrow a creature for 2 mana. Hauntwoods: a land
  every turn for 3 mana. The creature lane stays free for something else.
- **Muldrotha + Wistfulness / Mulldrifter / Shriekmaw / Night Incarnate.** Evoke
  from the yard every turn. Wistfulness paid with GG exiles an artifact or
  enchantment; paid with UU it draws 2 and discards 1.
- **Twilight Diviner.** The first creature each turn that we cast or reanimate from
  the yard gets a token copy: two Chupacabras, two Mulldrifters, two Grave Titans.
- **Mystic Remora / Spore Frog / Seal of Primordium / Pernicious Deed loops.** Let
  each die or sacrifice it, then recast it next turn from the yard.
- **Land lane.** Fetchlands, horizon lands (Waterlogged Grove, Nurturing Peatland
  sacrifice to draw), Takenuma (channel regrowth), Boseiju and Otawara (channel
  removal) and Bojuka Bog all get replayed from the yard. Icetill Explorer and The
  Gitrog Monster add extra land drops.
- **Syr Konrad + Dreadhound + Massacre Wurm / Night Incarnate** against go-wide
  boards: every opposing token that dies drains each opponent twice, plus 2 life
  from its controller under the Wurm.

## Gems

None of these are on Muldrotha's EDHREC B3 page (2,567 decks; the page lists
cards down to about 1% inclusion).

- **Emeritus of Woe // Demonic Tutor.** A 5/4 for 4 that brings a Demonic Tutor
  every time Muldrotha recasts it, and it isn't a Game Changer. It beats Entomb
  and Buried Alive as the tutor slot because it repeats.
- **Emeritus of Ideation // Ancestral Recall.** A 5/5 flyer with ward 2 that draws
  3 for U on every cast. It beats Mulldrifter in the evasive-threat role.
  Mulldrifter stays in the deck as the cheap evoke loop.
- **Studious First-Year // Rampant Growth.** A one-drop that is ramp on turn 2 and
  ramp again on every recast. It beats Wood Elves and Farhaven Elf on curve.
- **Vastlands Scavenger // Bind to Life.** A 4/4 deathtouch for 3, plus a
  5-mana instant that mills 7 and puts a creature from among them into play. It
  is self-mill and reanimation in one card, and it does more than Satyr
  Wayfinder or Grisly Salvage.
- **Seedship Broodtender.** Mills 3 on ETB like Stitcher's Supplier. Later it
  sacrifices itself to reanimate, then Muldrotha recasts it. That makes it a
  2-drop reanimation engine.
- **Blossoming Tortoise.** Mills 3 and puts a land from the yard into play on ETB
  and on every attack. It is ramp and self-mill in one recurring card, where
  Sakura-Tribe Elder only ramps.
- **Overlord of the Balemurk / Overlord of the Hauntwoods.** The impending cost
  goes through the enchantment lane, which the crowd underuses. For 2 mana,
  mill 4 and regrow a creature, or for 3 mana, make a land, every turn. Each one
  turns into a 5/5 or 6/5 later.
- **Wistfulness.** A hybrid-evoke modal card: a 2-mana Disenchant or a 2-mana draw
  2 / discard 1 from the yard every turn. Hardcast, it does both on a 6/5. It is
  more flexible than Reclamation Sage.
- **Doom Whisperer.** A 6/6 flying trample for 5 that fills our own yard on demand.
  It is a better threat than Lord of Extinction or Jarad at the same slot.
- **Dreadhound.** A second Syr Konrad on a 6/6 body that mills 3 on ETB. Two drain
  engines make the "mill and drain" wincon real instead of incidental.
- **Skyfisher Spider.** Sacrifice a Supplier or Frog to Vindicate on ETB, every
  turn. Ravenous Chupacabra only hits creatures.
- **Night Incarnate.** Evoke from the yard for 4 each turn to get a recurring
  -3/-3 sweeper against token decks, which is what the B3 field is (see Testing).
  Our own small creatures come back. Muldrotha and our 6/6s survive it.

Staples kept because they're simply the best card for the slot: Sakura-Tribe Elder,
Eternal Witness, Kaya's Ghostform, Animate Dead, Spore Frog, Gravebreaker Lamia,
Seal of Primordium, Haywire Mite, Mulldrifter, Chupacabra, Shriekmaw, Pernicious
Deed, Toxic Deluge, Syr Konrad, The Gitrog Monster, Twilight Diviner, Doc Aurlock.

## Testing

**Goldfish** (`./edh analyze`, 10k trials, 36 lands):
- Land drops: T3 90%, T4 77%. Screw (≤2 lands on T4) 7%. Flood (≥9 lands by T7) 2%.
- Muldrotha: average first cast T5.7, on curve (T6) 73%, by T7 83%. Never
  color-blocked at +1.
- Curve: average nonland MV 3.22, with 14 five-drops. Of those, Mulldrifter,
  Shriekmaw, Wistfulness, Night Incarnate and both Overlords have 2–4 mana
  alternate costs, so the real top end is about 8 cards at 5 plus 4 at 6+.
- Sample hands: 4 of 6 keepable, 2 one-landers (mulligans).

**Forge sim** (`sims/20260923-044115-b3`, 24 games vs the B3 gauntlet, run on
`candidates/deck-v1-simmed.txt`):
- **0 wins in 24 (95% CI 0–14%; baseline 25%).** One timeout.
- Average game length 10.7 rounds. We died on round 10.6, all 22 losses to life
  total, and 100% of the damage we took was combat damage. The biggest sources
  were Edgar's Vampire tokens, Hearthhull's Fungus Dinosaur tokens, Sauron's Orc
  Army and Nazgûl, and commanders.
- Muldrotha was cast in 92% of games, first on round 6.8.
- **The sim is mostly blind to this deck's engine.** I tracked card zones through
  the logs. The AI cast roughly **28 nonland spells from the graveyard in 24
  games, about 1 per game**. These were mostly Chupacabra, Wistfulness evoke, Doom
  Whisperer and Binding the Old Gods. In real play Muldrotha casts 2–4 things from
  the yard every turn she's out. So the sim measured the 99 as a fair midrange pile
  with a 6-mana 6/6 on top. The loops the list is built around (Remora, Frog,
  Seals, evoke creatures, Overlord impending, Emeritus recasts) almost never
  happened. That's the main reason to read the result as a floor, not a verdict.
  It likely applies to every Muldrotha build in this folder.
- **Prepared spells.** The AI cast Ancestral Recall ×4, Rampant Growth ×5 and Bind
  to Life ×1. It cast Emeritus of Woe but **never cast its prepared Demonic Tutor**,
  so the sim also undervalues that card. Twilight Diviner copies did fire (for
  example, two Overlords of the Hauntwoods).
- **Never cast.** Toxic Deluge: flagged `ai_cannot_play`, so ignore it. Skyfisher
  Spider: the AI apparently won't evaluate the optional sacrifice-then-reflexive-target
  line. Keep it; it's a Vindicate on a body.
- Other AI-weak flags: Mystic Remora, Survival of the Fittest, Pernicious Deed,
  Secrets of the Dead, Living Death (`ai_weak`), and Command Beacon
  (`ai_cannot_play`).
- **What I changed after the sim (v1 → final, not re-simmed; budget spent).** The
  gauntlet killed us with go-wide token boards. The deck had 3 wipes, and the AI
  can't cast one of them (Deluge) and plays two of them poorly (Deed, Living
  Death).
  - Anchovy & Banana Pizza out, **Massacre Wurm** in. The Pizza's loop cost 6 mana
    per creature kill and duplicated Chupacabra. The Wurm is a one-sided sweeper
    that also drains, and it's recurrable.
  - Sidisi, Brood Tyrant out, **Night Incarnate** in. Sidisi was a marginal
    three-color 4-drop. Night Incarnate is a recurring sweeper against exactly
    what beat us.
  - One Island became a Swamp. Black is 46% of pips, and Massacre Wurm is BBB.
    Sources are now U 20 / B 24 / G 23.
  - These are 2 cards, below what a sim can resolve anyway. I justify them on
    reasoning, not on a win rate.

**Honest read.** I expect this deck to play well above what the sim shows,
because Muldrotha's recursion is what the list is built around. That is a
reasoned expectation, not a measurement: Forge can't test it. The sim does say the deck is slow and reactive when the engine isn't
running, and that the B3 field attacks with wide boards. If Muldrotha is answered
repeatedly, expect a grindy midrange game in which the tutors and evoke
creatures still work from hand.

## Bracket

`./edh validate deck.txt --bracket 3` → **PASS**.
- Game Changers: 2 of 3 (Rhystic Study, Survival of the Fittest). The third slot is
  deliberately open. The deck has repeatable tutoring already (Emeritus of Woe,
  Lamia, Survival), and Demonic Tutor or The One Ring would push it toward B4 feel.
- Combos: Commander Spellbook found **no combos** in the 99. The deck-level tag
  "Powerful (≈bracket 3+)" is its generic estimate. No two-card infinite exists.
  - Kaya's Ghostform + Animate Dead style loops don't go infinite.
  - Aftermath Analyst + Muldrotha is value, not a loop.
- MLD: none. **Strip Mine / Wasteland were deliberately left out.** Muldrotha
  replaying a Strip Mine every turn is a land-destruction lock, which is against
  the spirit of B3.
- Extra turns: none.
- Spirit check: the fastest realistic "win" is Reanimate on a milled Grave-Reaver
  or Grave Titan on turn 2–3. It's possible, but it needs the fatty milled, and
  neither creature wins on its own. There's no Entomb or Buried Alive, per the
  brief. Board dominance on turns 6–8 is the normal pattern, which matches B3.

## Swaps considered (flex slots, for the user's taste)

- **Open Game Changer slot.** The One Ring (a protection turn plus draw, strong
  against the go-wide field) or Demonic Tutor. Cut Underrealm Lich or Kheru
  Goldkeeper for it.
- **More artifact-lane removal.** Executioner's Capsule (3 mana per loop,
  nonblack only) or Anchovy & Banana Pizza (cut in v2). Seal of Doom for the
  enchantment lane.
- **Sheoldred, Whispering One / Sludge Titan / Chupacabra Echo.** All good. They
  missed on curve (too many 5–7s already).
- **Overlord of the Floodpits.** The third impending Overlord (draw 2, discard 1
  for 3). A good swap for Underrealm Lich if you want more lane diversity.
- **River Kelpie.** A body for Secrets of the Dead. It draws off every yard cast
  and every reanimation. Swap for Secrets if you want the body.
- **Utility lands: Port of Karfell, Memorial to Folly.** Mill and reanimate, or
  regrow, and Muldrotha replays them. Left out because they enter tapped. Swap
  for Exotic Orchard if the table is slow.
- **Altar of Dementia** (40% of the crowd). A free sac outlet for Supplier and
  Broodtender deaths. Left out because we don't mill opponents and it doesn't
  affect the board. Forge also can't play it.
- **Sidisi, Brood Tyrant** (cut in v2), **Grisly Salvage**, **Life from the Loam**,
  **Hermit Druid.** The last is rejected because it sits in combo territory (see
  strategies.md).
- **Rejected on purpose:** Entomb, Buried Alive (brief); Strip Mine loops (MLD
  spirit); Tithing Blade (the edict artifact never goes back to the yard, so
  Muldrotha can't loop it); the dozen "mill 3 on ETB" bears that Jev ranks
  highly (Necromancer's Assistant, Armored Skaab, Crow of Dark Tidings). Each
  does less than Seedship Broodtender or Satyr Wayfinder.
