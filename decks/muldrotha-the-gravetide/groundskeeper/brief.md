# Muldrotha, the Gravetide: Groundskeeper (Bracket 3)

Commander: Muldrotha, the Gravetide ({3}{B}{G}{U}, 6/6). "During each of your turns, you may play a land and cast a permanent spell of each permanent type from your graveyard."

Thesis: The land lane is Muldrotha's free lane. Every turn we can play a land from the graveyard instead of from hand, so any land that sacrifices itself for value becomes a repeatable effect. (Rules note, added by the builder: a land played from the graveyard uses a normal land play. Muldrotha grants permission, not an extra drop. Extra-land-drop effects and effects that put lands from the graveyard directly onto the battlefield, such as Undergrowth Recon, Lumra and Blossoming Tortoise, are what turn the lane into ramp. Muldrotha allows one land from the graveyard per turn. Ramunap, Crucible, Mole Man and Icetill let every available land drop come from the graveyard, so they still matter alongside extra drops once Muldrotha is out.) Fetch lands give ramp and two landfall triggers each turn. Horizon lands and cycling lands draw a card each turn. Sacrifice lands give recurring utility. Before Muldrotha, extra-land-drop creatures and graveyard-land recursion (Life from the Loam, Ramunap Excavator, Crucible effects) run the same engine, so the deck works without its commander. Muldrotha adds lanes on top.

Win conditions: Landfall token armies (Avenger of Zendikar, Scute Swarm, Rampaging Baloths style), big landfall-pumped boards, and Field of the Dead if it's taken as a Game Changer. The target is to take over by turns 7–9 with a wide board that one wipe doesn't end, because the lands keep coming back.

Engine:
- Sacrifice-to-value lands: fetch lands (true fetches, Evolving Wilds, Fabled Passage, Prismatic Vista), horizon lands in our colors (Nurturing Peatland, Waterlogged Grove), cycling lands (Tranquil Thicket, Lonely Sandbar, Barren Moor, Fetid Pools, Zagoth Triome), and lands with channel or sacrifice abilities.
- Graveyard-land recursion before Muldrotha: Life from the Loam, Ramunap Excavator, Crucible of Worlds, Conduit of Worlds, World Shaper, Six, Ancient Greenwarden, Splendid Reclamation.
- Extra land drops: Azusa, Oracle of Mul Daya, Dryad of the Ilysian Grove, Exploration, Burgeoning, Aesi, Wayward Swordtooth.
- Landfall payoffs: Tatyova, Lotus Cobra, Tireless Tracker, Avenger of Zendikar, Scute Swarm, Titania (Protector of Argoth), Retreat to Coralhelm, Rampaging Baloths.
- Self-mill that hits lands (it fuels the graveyard lane): Hedge Maze-style surveil lands, World Shaper, Stitcher's Supplier, Satyr Wayfinder.

Wants: Permanents that sacrifice themselves or lands for value; land-based card draw; landfall that yields cards or tokens rather than +1/+1 only; cheap ways to discard or mill lands (discard outlets that key off lands); resilient threats that rebuild after a wipe.

Avoid: Graveyard hate that hits our own yard (Bojuka Bog is fine: we recur it against opponents). Symmetrical land destruction (Armageddon-style) is out: it's bracket-illegal. Repeated Strip Mine or Wasteland recursion on opponents every turn is a soft land lock, so at most one such land, used as an answer to utility lands, not as a plan. Glacial Chasm loops are a Game Changer and a lock: avoid unless the deck clearly needs a defensive trick. Skip plain +1/+1 landfall pumps with no card or token output.

Interaction plan: Recurring removal via channel lands and lands with activated removal; a few flexible spells (Assassin's Trophy, Pongify-style, counters like Mystic Snake); 2–3 board wipes we can rebuild from (Toxic Deluge, Pernicious Deed, Crux of Fate). Protect the engine against graveyard hate with instant-speed tricks or redundancy, not by relying on one piece.

Bracket constraints: Bracket 3. At most 3 Game Changers; candidates that fit the plan are Field of the Dead, Crop Rotation, Worldly Tutor and Survival of the Fittest. No two-card early infinite combos, no mass land denial.

Targets: 38–40 lands (lands are spells here), ramp 10+ including extra-land-drop effects, card draw ~10 (land-based draw counts), removal 8, wipes 2–3, protection 2–3.

User preferences: none given (demo run). Proxies, so no budget limit.
