# Sephiroth, Fabled SOLDIER

PLAN: Floods the board with recursive fodder (Reassembling Skeleton, Bloodghast, Gravecrawler, Bitterblossom), sacrifices it for cards and drain, and edicts us out with Grave Pact and Marauders.

ENGINE: Sephiroth drains 1 whenever any other creature dies. On the fourth drain in a turn it transforms, flies, and grants a permanent drain emblem. Key cards: Blood Artist, Zulaport Cutthroat, Pitiless Plunderer, Ashnod's Altar, Skullclamp.

KILL ON SIGHT: Pitiless Plunderer, Ashnod's Altar, Grave Pact. Plunderer with Gravecrawler, a Zombie and Viscera Seer is an infinite loop. So is Skeleton with Altar and Plunderer. Exile recursive bodies, since destroying them just fuels drains. Once it transforms, the emblem stays, so killing Sephiroth does NOT stop it. Kill it before four deaths.

PUNISHES: Any creature dying, ours included, such as chump blocks, our wipes and our edicts.

EXPECT: Toxic Deluge, Feed the Swarm, Flare of Malice and repeated edicts. Keep cheap fodder around. Reanimate and Victimize use graveyards, so graveyard hate hurts it.

CLOCK: A combo loop around turns 5-7. Otherwise a grinding drain.
