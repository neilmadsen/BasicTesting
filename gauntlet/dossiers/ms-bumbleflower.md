# Ms. Bumbleflower

PLAN: Ramps with Sol Ring, Signet, Farseek and Cultivate, then chains cheap spells through Bumbleflower. Every second trigger in a turn draws it two cards. It wins by stacking counters on one creature and flying it in.

ENGINE: Bumbleflower (the second resolution each turn draws two). Also Managorger Hydra, Forgotten Ancient, Chasm Skulker, Jolrael, Faerie Mastermind and Twenty-Toed Toad.

KILL ON SIGHT: Bumbleflower. Destroy works, and it's usually booted with Swiftfoot Boots, so prefer wipes or edicts. Killing it stops the draw engine, but the Hydra and Ancient keep growing. Exile Chasm Skulker, since its death spits out islandwalk Squids.

PUNISHES: Our spells grow the Hydra and Ancient. The "free" cards from its triggers and gifts fuel Mastermind. Killing Skulker makes tokens.

EXPECT: Swords, Path, Generous Gift, Loran, Wear Down, Offer You Can't Refuse, Long River's Pull and a Promise of Loyalty wipe. Bait counters with medium spells, and use its Offer Treasures.

CLOCK: Turns 8-10, through combat with one oversized flier or trampling Hydra.
