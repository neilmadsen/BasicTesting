# Lathril, Blade of the Elves

PLAN: Mana elves into lords, then flood the board with Elf tokens and win with one big turn: Lathril's 10-drain, Craterhoof Behemoth, or an Ezuri, Renegade Leader overrun.

ENGINE: Lathril drains 10 by tapping ten Elves; with Dionus, Elvish Archdruid out, the tapped Elves can untap for a second drain. Dangerous cards: Elvish Archdruid, Priest of Titania, Beast Whisperer, Elvish Warmaster, Elven Ambush/Elvish Promenade, Craterhoof.

KILL ON SIGHT: Lathril, since the drain lives on it; destroy works because it has no recursion. Also Priest of Titania, Elvish Archdruid, Beast Whisperer, Elderfang Venom and Poison-Tip Archer. Killing Lathril does NOT stop Craterhoof or Shaman of the Pack.

PUNISHES: Any creature death drains us with Poison-Tip Archer (ours too) or Elderfang Venom (theirs), so wipe only when Venom is gone. Unblocked Lathril makes tokens, so double-block menace. Attacks into deathtouch Archer or Tyvar Kell cost us creatures.

EXPECT: Assassin's Trophy, Reclamation Sage and Heroic Intervention. Bait Intervention before your wipe, or wipe when they're tapped out.

CLOCK: Turns 5–7, usually from a drain or a Hoof/Ezuri alpha.
