# Hearthhull, the Worldseed

PLAN: Ramps hard (Nature's Lore, Farseek, Lotus Cobra), then sacrifices lands for drain and card draw. It refills the graveyard with Splendid Reclamation, Ramunap Excavator or Worldsoul's Rage and sacrifices again.

ENGINE: Hearthhull pings each opponent for 2 per land sacrificed, even unstationed. At 2+ counters it sacrifices lands to draw two. Key pieces: Zuran Orb (free, repeatable land sacrifice), The Gitrog Monster, Korvold, Mayhem Devil, Splendid Reclamation.

KILL ON SIGHT: Hearthhull. Below 8 counters it's a noncreature artifact, so use artifact removal and exile it if possible. Exile or counter Zuran Orb. Killing Hearthhull does NOT stop Gitrog, Braids, Tannuk or Mayhem Devil.

PUNISHES: Our sacrifices feed Mayhem Devil. Land destruction on them just stocks Reclamation and Ramunap. Attacking into deathtouch Gitrog trades badly.

EXPECT: Blasphemous Act (hold back creatures), Beast Within, and kicked Tear Asunder (exile). Sylvan Safekeeper shrouds creatures, so target Hearthhull before it stations to 8.

CLOCK: Turns 6-8. Drain bursts come from Orb plus Reclamation, backed by Omnath tokens and Korvold in the air.
