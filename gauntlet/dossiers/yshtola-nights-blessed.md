# Y'shtola, Night's Blessed

PLAN: Slow Esper control. It hides behind Propaganda and Ghostly Prison, trades removal one-for-one, and drains everyone 2 at a time before finishing with a big Exsanguinate.

ENGINE: Y'shtola pings each opponent for 2 on every noncreature spell with MV 3+, and draws if anyone lost 4+ life. Key cards: Papalymo Totolymo, Kambal, Emet-Selch (recasts spells from graveyard when we lose life), and Curiosity, Ophidian Eye or Helm of the Ghastlord on vigilant Y'shtola.

KILL ON SIGHT: Y'shtola. Exile or tuck it; it has no eminence, so removing it stops the engine. Also Emet-Selch and Kambal. Counter Exsanguinate.

PUNISHES: Our noncreature spells (Kambal), attacking into Propaganda taxes, and its own life payments (Snuff Out, Dismember), which trigger its card draw.

EXPECT: Heavy spot removal: Swords, Snuff Out (black creatures are safe), Dismember, Deadly Rollick, Void Rend, Vindicate, Anguished Unmaking. Unwind only counters noncreature spells. No sweepers are listed, so spread threats and lead with creatures.

CLOCK: Slow, around turns 9-11. It wins by drain plus an Exsanguinate finisher.
