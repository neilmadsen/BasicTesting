# Edgar Markov

PLAN: Floods the board with 1/1 Vampire tokens from every Vampire cast, pumps them with lords, then swings wide with first strike or drains everyone out. It can also win on the spot with a lifegain combo.

ENGINE: Eminence makes tokens from the command zone, so it works even if Edgar is never cast. The key cards are Captivating Vampire, Stromkirk Captain, Cordial Vampire, Skullclamp, Sanctum Seeker, and Vito paired with Exquisite Blood or Bloodthirsty Conqueror, which is an infinite drain.

KILL ON SIGHT: Remove Vito, Exquisite Blood (needs enchantment removal), Bloodthirsty Conqueror and Skullclamp immediately. Exile Elenda; destroying her leaves tokens. Killing Edgar does NOT stop the tokens.

PUNISHES: Any creature dying (Blood Artist, Cruel Celebrant, Cordial Vampire), so avoid chump-blocking. Our creatures entering untapped (Charismatic Conqueror). With Bloodthirsty Conqueror out, every point of life we lose heals it.

EXPECT: Swords to Plowshares, Path to Exile, Anguished Unmaking, a one-sided Olivia's Wrath, and theft from Captivating Vampire. It runs no counters. Don't overcommit non-Vampires, and wipe only after the drainers are gone.

CLOCK: Lethal in combat around turns 6-8, or instantly once the Vito combo lands.
