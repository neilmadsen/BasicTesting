# Sauron, the Dark Lord

PLAN: Ramps into Sauron (Sol Ring, Arcane Signet, Relic of Sauron) and grows an Orc Army off our spells. Army hits wheel it for four, and the discards fuel Reanimate and Living Death.

ENGINE: Sauron's amass and Ring-tempt wheel. Key cards: Call of the Ring (a wheel every upkeep), Saruman, the White Hand, Mauhúr, The Great Goblin, The One Ring.

KILL ON SIGHT: Call of the Ring, Mauhúr and Great Goblin can all be destroyed. The One Ring needs exile or bounce. Sauron's ward demands a legendary sacrifice, so counter, edict or wipe him. Killing him stops the wheel, but the Army and graveyard remain, so exile their yard before Living Death.

PUNISHES: Every spell we cast (Army counter, +1 with Mauhúr, 2 damage via Great Goblin). Extra draws feed Orcish Bowmasters. Hitting him with Witch-king out costs those attackers. Our dead creatures become Reanimate targets.

EXPECT: Blasphemous Act, Feed the Swarm, Arcane Denial, Saruman's Trickery, flashed Bowmasters. Don't overextend. Cast fewer, bigger spells.

CLOCK: Turns 6-8, via Army/Wraith combat plus Great Goblin pings.
