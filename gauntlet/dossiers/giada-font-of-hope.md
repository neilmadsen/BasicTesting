# Giada, Font of Hope

PLAN: Giada ramps and stacks counters so each Angel lands bigger than the last. Lifegain grows the flying board, and Akroma's Will ends it with double strike.

ENGINE: Giada, plus Archangel of Thune, Righteous Valkyrie, Resplendent Angel, Starnheim Aspirant/Herald of War (cost cuts) and Avacyn.

KILL ON SIGHT: Giada early; she's cheap. Thune: use Path, not Swords, since Swords' lifegain triggers Thune. Avacyn needs exile or bounce. Killing Giada does NOT stop Aspirant/Incubator discounts or Bruna/Emeria Shepherd recursion.

PUNISHES: Any lifegain we give (Swords). Attacking into untapped Archangel of Tithes. Killing Angels with Bishop of Wings out makes Spirits. Drawing into Smothering Tithe. Sac outlets are dead with Angel of Jubilation, and activated creature abilities with Linvala.

EXPECT: Swords, Path, and free Flawless Maneuver while Giada lives. No counterspells. Remove Giada first, then wipe with exile/bounce.

CLOCK: Turns 6-8 at casual-focused tables, through evasive combat. Akroma's Will can end it a turn early.
