# Pantlaza, Sun-Favored

PLAN: Ramp (Farseek, Cultivate, Topiary Stomper) into Pantlaza, then chain big-toughness Dinosaurs for free discover casts. It wins with a hasty, trampling Dinosaur alpha strike.

ENGINE: Pantlaza discovers X (the entering Dinosaur's toughness) once per turn. The key cards are Regisaur Alpha (haste), Rhythm of the Wild (uncounterable creatures), Wrathful Raptors, Ghalta, Stampede Tyrant, and Trumpeting Carnosaur, which discovers 5 on its own.

KILL ON SIGHT: Destroy Wrathful Raptors immediately. Remove Rhythm of the Wild with enchantment removal, since once it's out, creature counters stop working. Counter or kill Pantlaza early. Killing it does stop discover (no eminence), and destroy is enough because commander tax does the work.

PUNISHES: Damaging its Dinosaurs while Raptors is out. Blocks, burn and fights all get redirected at us. Apex Altisaur's enrage chains fights.

EXPECT: Wakening Sun's Avatar wipes all non-Dinosaurs, but only when cast from hand, not when discovered or put in by Ghalta. It also runs Path to Exile, Bronzebeak Foragers, and Apex Altisaur fights. Don't overcommit creatures.

CLOCK: Turns 6-8 via combat, with trample, haste and Akroma's Will double strike.
