# Atraxa, Praetors' Voice

PLAN: Ramps (Sol Ring, Farseek, Cultivate, Astral Cornucopia) into stacked proliferators. It seeds poison with Prologue to Phyresis, Infectious Inquiry, Vraska's Fall and toxic creatures, then proliferates everyone to 10.

ENGINE: Atraxa proliferates each end step. Tekuthal doubles every proliferate, Inexorable Tide and Flux Channeler proliferate on each spell, Ezuri draws per proliferate, Vorinclex doubles counters, and Vraska, Betrayal's Sting ramps loyalty.

KILL ON SIGHT: Tekuthal and Inexorable Tide first, then Vorinclex and Vraska. Destroy works on Atraxa, since it has no death trigger or eminence. Exile adds recast tax. Killing Atraxa does NOT stop Tide, Channeler or Evolution Sage.

PUNISHES: Every poison counter is permanent and grows each turn, so block toxic creatures and Thrummingbird. Don't attack into or block Atraxa, which has deathtouch, lifelink and vigilance. Our own counters won't help us.

EXPECT: Swords, Path, Counterspell and Vraska's Fall edicts. Keep a spare creature, and bait counters before your key spell.

CLOCK: Turns 7-9 via poison proliferation. Life totals barely matter, so track poison.
