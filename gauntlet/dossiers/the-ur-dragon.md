# The Ur-Dragon

PLAN: Ramps hard (Sol Ring, Signet, Farseek) into cheap Dragons, then swings with hasty flyers. It wins through combat and burn from Dragons entering the battlefield.

ENGINE: The Ur-Dragon's eminence discounts Dragons, and each attack draws cards and puts a permanent into play free. Key cards: Miirym (copies), Terror of the Peaks, Scourge of Valkas, Dragon Tempest, Lathliss, Dracogenesis.

KILL ON SIGHT: Kill Terror of the Peaks and Scourge of Valkas right away. Exile Miirym, paying ward {2}. Counter Dracogenesis. Under Call the Spirit Dragons, only exile or bounce works. Killing The Ur-Dragon does NOT stop the discount, and Hellkite Courser brings it back with haste.

PUNISHES: Targeting Terror of the Peaks costs 3 life, and targeting Goldspan Dragon gives it a Treasure. Your low-toughness creatures and planeswalkers die to Dragons entering. Ground blockers do nothing against flyers.

EXPECT: Crux of Fate on "non-Dragons" (one-sided wipe). Dromoka stops spells on its turn, so cast instants at its end step. Rhythm of the Wild makes its creatures uncounterable, so counter noncreature spells instead.

CLOCK: Turns 6-8, through Atarka's double strike, Utvara tokens, and burn from Dragons entering.
