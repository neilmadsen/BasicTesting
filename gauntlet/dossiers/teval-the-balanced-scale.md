# Teval, the Balanced Scale

PLAN: Ramps, then self-mills hard with Hedron Crab, Stitcher's Supplier and Six. It recurs lands and creatures from the graveyard to build a Zombie army and reanimate fatties. It wins by swinging with fliers.

ENGINE: Teval makes a 2/2 Zombie whenever cards leave its graveyard. Teval's Judgment, Life from the Loam, Muldrotha, The Gitrog Monster, Colossal Grave-Reaver and Icetill Explorer/Conduit of Worlds turn every land drop into value.

KILL ON SIGHT: Teval's Judgment and Insidious Roots (enchantment removal). Muldrotha and Gitrog (exile). Teval should be exiled or tucked. Killing Teval does NOT stop the engine: Judgment, Roots and Kheru Goldkeeper keep triggering, and destroyed creatures come back via Victimize, Dread Return or Muldrotha.

PUNISHES: One-at-a-time graveyard hate, since each removal event makes a token; exile the whole yard at once. Our dead fatties are Reanimate targets. Hedron Crab can mill us.

EXPECT: Assassin's Trophy, Tear Asunder, and Living Death as a board swap. Don't overcommit creatures while its yard is stacked. Hold instant-speed yard exile for Living Death.

CLOCK: Turns 7-9. Flying combat from Grave-Reaver and a Zombie swarm made evasive by Wonder.
