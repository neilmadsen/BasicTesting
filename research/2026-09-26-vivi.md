# A second deck: Vivi Ornitier

Follow-up to [the steer-mode note](2026-09-26-steer.md). Every pilot result so far had come from one deck,
Muldrotha. So the same tests were run with a very different one: the crowd's bracket 3 Vivi Ornitier
list, taken from EDHREC's bracket 3 average (3,000 decks).
- **One edit.** Curiosity became Resonating Lute, because Curiosity + Niv-Mizzet, Parun is an early
  two-card infinite, which bracket 3 excludes.
- **Brief.** A short one was written so the pilot has a plan.
- **Setup.** Same gauntlet, same pods, same seeds; every run is paired with the same deck's Forge-only run.

Data: `2026-09-26-vivi-data/`.

## Results

Differences are against Forge's AI playing the same deck on the same games. Negative means better than
Forge.

| | Muldrotha | Vivi |
|---|---|---|
| Forge's AI alone, avg place | 2.52 (seed 11) | 2.81 (seed 11), 2.50 (seed 12) |
| Jev pilot, static plan | +0.46 ± 0.19 (48 games) | **−0.21 ± 0.13** (94 games, two seeds) |
| steer, static plan | +0.06 ± 0.14 | |
| steer + cards Forge can't play | +0.21 ± 0.18 | **−0.17 ± 0.14** |

The two decks respond differently to the pilot. The difference is 0.67 ± 0.23 places, about 2.9 standard
errors.

## Why Vivi gains

Forge's AI never cast five of Vivi's cards, in 96 games over two seeds:
- Windfall;
- Faithless Looting;
- Izzet Signet;
- Gitaxian Probe;
- Mizzix's Mastery.

Forge's own card scripts flag these as cards its AI can't play. The pilot cast them about once a game.
In a spells deck, each cheap spell grows Vivi and pings every opponent. The pilot arm cast 15.4 spells a
game against Forge's 13.0, and got 5.2 Vivi triggers against 3.8.

**Steer mode with one added rule keeps nearly all of that gain.** The rule: Jev may play a card Forge's
AI can't, and choose its X and targets. Forge keeps every other decision.
- Overrules drop to 1% of questions.
- Placement is the same as the full pilot's: +0.06 ± 0.20 between the two.

## Why Muldrotha doesn't

Muldrotha's blocked cards are a different kind of card:
- Barrin, Master Wizard and Claws of Gix (sacrifice outlets);
- Toxic Deluge (a sweeper that hits our own board);
- Mishra's Bauble;
- The One Ring.

The pilot used them about 1.4 times a game, mostly Barrin and Bauble activations and Claws of Gix. That
arm placed +0.17 ± 0.13 worse than plain steer mode. It also hit 21 loop-breakers, more than any other
arm.

That is not significant. It fits a sensible reading, though. Forge's developers flag cards for two
different reasons:
- **Its AI can't see the value.** Card flow like Windfall and Faithless Looting. Unlocking these is pure
  gain.
- **Its AI would misuse them.** Sacrifice outlets and symmetrical sweepers. These need judgement a static
  plan doesn't give Jev.

## What this says

- **Forge's AI is a reasonable tactician on the cards it understands.** The pilot's clearest value is
  playing the cards it doesn't.
- **That value depends on how central those cards are to the deck.**
  - For Vivi they are the engine.
  - For Muldrotha they are a few strong but tricky singletons.
- **Muldrotha's weak Forge baseline isn't the pilot's to fix.** The graveyard-permission play that Forge
  handles badly didn't improve when Jev took over. Every Muldrotha arm that overrules Forge more than
  occasionally lost placement.
- **"Is the commander strong?" gets a nuanced answer.** In Forge the crowd's Vivi places worse than our
  Muldrotha (2.81 against 2.52 on seed 11). Seed 12 put Vivi at 2.50. With the pilot unlocking its cards,
  Vivi is about even with Muldrotha. The gauntlet's best decks in Forge are the linear creature and drain
  decks (Y'shtola 50%, Giada 47%, Edgar 44% win rates, against a 25% par), because those are what Forge's
  AI plays well.
