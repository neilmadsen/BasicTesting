## The losses, up close

**pod03 g1, 3rd: decked on the turn we had a guaranteed win.**
- **Board.** At turn 33 we were on 23 life with a 48-card hand and 15 cards in library after the draw. Vivi was
  19/22. The two opponents were on 21 and 24, fully tapped out.
- **The memo said "Win now".** Its script was seven cheap noncreature spells to grow Vivi, then her mana into
  Crackle with Power X=5 (17 mana, 25 damage to each opponent).
- **What it missed.** Tandem Lookout was paired with Vivi, and Harmonic Prodigy doubles both her ping and the
  Lookout's draw trigger. So each noncreature spell drew 8 cards (2 pings × 2 opponents × 2 draws). Mystic Remora
  drew 8 and left 7. Sol Ring then drew from an empty library.
- **The real line.** With 11 mana from lands plus 19 from Vivi, Crackle X=5 as the first spell kills both. Forge's
  own pick, Chandra's Ignition, also won that turn through combat. Jev overruled it at 0.79 for the memo's plan.
- **What the memo knew.** It had the library count ("don't cast Windfall or extra draw"). It had no way to see the
  pairing, which the state doesn't show.

**pod04 g1, 2nd: a scripted win that was never a win.** Heads-up against Giada (23 flying power, Avacyn hexproof
under Swiftfoot Boots), on 13 life. The memo scripted a Hullbreaker Horror loop, returning Thought Vessel and Fiery
Inscription in turn, then a 9-power Ring-bearer Vivi "unblockable".
- **The rule is misread.** The Ring stops blockers with *greater* power, so every creature P2 had could block her.
- **Forge chose Hullbreaker's trigger.** It bounced P2's Emeria Shepherd, not our Thought Vessel, so the loop never
  started. The pilot doesn't route triggered-ability modes or targets at all.
- **Jev carried on with the dead script.** It activated Vivi with an empty hand, and attacked with a 4/7 Vivi into
  four blockers.
- **The game was already lost.** It was lost the turn before, to a 14-point flying swing.

**pod05 g1, 4th** (see the earlier micro notes):
- Cascade Bluffs was never used to filter for Niv-Mizzet.
- A general "No attacks" in the memo didn't reach every attacker.
- Frantic Search's discard went to Forge.
- We got no priority with our own spell on the stack.

## Found and fixed

  "play Mountain and cast Vivi".

## Open, for the next ten

**Harness:**
1. Triggered-ability modes and targets go to Forge. This matters most for Hullbreaker Horror, and for any "choose
   one" trigger.
2. Filter lands (Cascade Bluffs) as explicit mana actions.
3. Vivi's colour split, sized to the most expensive castable spell.
4. A general "No attacks" should apply to every attacker.
5. Effect discards (Frantic Search) should go to Jev and the memo.
6. Priority with our own spell on the stack, when we have instants.
7. The equipment cap should be 2 moves per turn, so a move-and-return is possible.
8. Command Tower's colours in the hand summary.
9. Soulbond pairing in the state.

**Executor:**
- Later steps of a script stay tagged after an earlier step fails. They should lose their tags, or escalate
  to a quick re-plan.

**Strategist:**
- **Count every draw trigger a plan fires** when the library is small, and prefer the shortest kill.
- **Check keyword rules before resting a win on them** (the Ring's evasion).
- **Keep a found combo plan.**
- **Read "that player" wording** before putting auras or equipment on an opponent's creature (Sigil of Sleep).
