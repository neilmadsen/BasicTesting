# Ten Vivi games with Opus planning every turn

Follow-up to [the Vivi note](2026-09-26-vivi.md). This run is the first with every harness fix that single
games exposed:
- plan tags that match nicknames, quote the step, and mark fallbacks (29c51a4);
- holds that no longer hide plays (be0143d);
- transient API errors retried (ac1b33e);
- Vivi's mana as an explicit action, with overload-aware tags (530075c).

**Setup.** The crowd's bracket 3 Vivi list, 10 games (seed 11, five pods of two), three arms:
- Opus plans at the start of each of our turns and Jev decides everything;
- Jev on the static brief;
- Forge's AI alone.

Strategist mulligans (9dab807) came after the launch and aren't in this run. Data: `2026-09-28-vivi10-data/`.

## What the games showed

**pod05 g1: a win on turn 25.**
- Vivi's mana grew over four consecutive turns of ours: 6, 8, 16, 22.
- Sauron died to pings, Pantlaza to a hexproof 28/31 Vivi, and Teval to Grapeshot after Slip Out the Back
  phased out their deathtouch blocker.
- The memos counted trigger multipliers (Harmonic Prodigy plus Wizard's Staff), aimed Niv-Mizzet's pings, and
  warned against phasing out our own engines.

**pod01 g1: lost to a crack-back, with the opponent on 1 life.** The memo sent every creature at a player it
predicted would survive ("P2 to about 3"). The opponent then looped Reassembling Skeleton into Carrion Feeder
and killed us from 8.

**pod05 g2: lost to a keep and a wasted counter.**
- Jev kept Island, Island plus five spells, three of which need red, and we found red on the fourth land.
- Our only counterspell went on Orcish Bowmasters. The tag had presented "Hold {1}{U}{U} for Mana Sculpt…"
  as a planned play, and Forge wanted to cast it.
- Two turns later Gishath built a 47-power board that nobody at the table answered.
- Given the same hand, the strategist mulligans it.

**Y'shtola, the Esper drain deck, won 4 of the 6 games watched at pod 1.** The memos kept ranking it last:
"slow", "leave them alone".

## Known executor gaps

1. **Holds read as plans.** A card named in "hold X for Y" is tagged as a planned play, with no earmark shown.
2. **Refusing Forge's play for a held card needs the big margin (0.35).** This wasted An Offer You Can't Refuse
   on a mana rock, and Mana Sculpt on Bowmasters.
3. **Upkeep impulses.** Off-plan plays in our own upkeep spend mana the plan needs; Fire Magic did this.
4. **Attack instructions in THIS TURN aren't tagged.** Only threat rank reaches attack options.
5. **Equipment ping-pong.** Lightning Greaves moved 17 times between two creatures in one main phase, because
   the plan step stays tagged after it's done.

## Strategist gaps

- **All-in attacks that aren't lethal,** at low life, into a crack-back.
- **Control and drain opponents rated as "slow".**
